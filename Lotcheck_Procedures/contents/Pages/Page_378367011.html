8 Notification Email From NDP

NDP issues various notification emails in response to user operations and internal processing. These emails are summarized in this list.

The emails are sent to not just report information, but may also require user operations or handling. Handle issues as soon as possible. Handling delays may lead to delays with Lotcheck.

Contents

  • Description
  • List of Notification Emails
    • [Needs Response] A thread was created or a post was submitted to a thread by the developer, Nintendo, or NDP automatic processing　(Messages tab or Lotcheck Issues Report).
    • A post was submitted to the thread or the thread status was changed by the developer, Nintendo, or NDP automatic processing (Issues or Lotcheck Issues Report).
    • A post was submitted to a thread that does not affect Lotcheck Judgment, by the developer, Nintendo, or NDP automatic processing (Messages tab or Lotcheck Issues).
    • A waiver was requested or judgment results are available.
    • Developer canceled Lotcheck
    • Upload complete after uploading the ROM
    • After the ROM was uploaded, an error occurred and upload did not complete
    • Lotcheck Judgment was NG
    • Lotcheck Judgment Was Canceled
    • Lotcheck Judgment was Suspended
    • Lotcheck Judgment was OK
    • Developer revised a page on the Release Dashboard
    • Lotcheck Judgment reverted for some reason

Description

  • The developer must post a response to the thread for any email that contains "回答必要" or "Need Response" in the subject line.
  • Also, email may be sent to developers as a result of developer operations. The expectation is that this will facilitate communication between developers.
  • Email is sent to the users and user groups registered in the Members tab of the associated product, and the related parties at Nintendo.
  • The body of the email contains a summary of the issue and a link to NDP.

List of Notification Emails

[Needs Response] A thread was created or a post was submitted to a thread by the developer, Nintendo, or NDP automatic processing
　(Messages tab or Lotcheck Issues Report).

Issues Handled by the Developer

Access the thread using the URL described in the email and check the thread contents. Select the response and resolution method.

  • You can only respond to the message in the thread.
  • Select the resolution method from the Issues page for the applicable release.

Email Subject Example

Japanese environment: [NDP][HAC-P-ABCDE][回答必要] Question A

English environment: [NDP][HAC-P-ABCDE][Needs Response] Question A

Rules for Generating the Email Subject

Japanese environment: [NDP] + [game code] + [回答必要] + thread title

English environment: [NDP] + [game code] + [Needs Response] + thread title
