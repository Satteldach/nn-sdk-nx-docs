9 Contacts for Inquiries Before, During, and After Lotcheck Testing

This page describes the methods for developers to contact Nintendo.

When possible, do not send an email, but use the thread in the Messages tab on NDP. This shares the information faster with the relevant parties within Nintendo.

NDP Messages Tab

Can be used regardless of the content of the inquiry.

Related NDP Pages

NDP > My Products > target_product > Messages tab

Procedure
  1. Select the Messages tab.
  2. Click + New Thread to create a new thread.
    Use this feature with reference to the Help on the screen.

Inquiry Email Addresses

On Nintendo Developer Portal, see the contacts page (https://developer.nintendo.com/group/development/contact).

The email address is configured according to the contents of the inquiry.
