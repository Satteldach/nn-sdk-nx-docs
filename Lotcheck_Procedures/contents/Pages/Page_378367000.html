4.4 Checking Compliance With Guidelines

It is hard work to list all of the guidelines the application should comply with based on the application's specifications. This process is assisted by NDP.

On NDP, the Guidelines page lists the items the application must comply with, based on the entries made on the Features page and the information detected in ROM Upload.
It also describes how to verify compliance with each guideline.

A description of that process is presented here.

Checking Guideline Compliance

Review the list of guideline items and verify that your application complies with each item.

Related NDP Pages
  • NDP > MY PRODUCTS > target_product > Releases or Downloadable Content > target_release > go to the guide on the right side of the screen > Features
  • NDP > MY PRODUCTS > target_product > Releases or Downloadable Content > target_release > go to the guide on the right side of the screen > Guidelines
  • NDP > MY PRODUCTS > target_product > Releases or Downloadable Content > target_release > go to the guide on the right side of the screen > ROM Upload
Procedure
  1. Finish entering information for the items on the Features page, as described in 4.2 Entering Features.

  2. Click Guidelines. A list of guidelines that require compliance is displayed based on the entries made in Features.


    Note: This video does not have audio.

  3. Make sure you comply with all guidelines before submitting a master ROM. The procedure for checking compliance is listed, as shown in the screen in step 2.
  4. When compliance is confirmed, select the check box at the bottom of the page and click Save.


  5. When ROM Upload is performed, NDP detects the information included in the ROM. Depending on the results, items might be added to the guidelines items.
    If something was added, perform steps 2 to 4.


Note:

If compliance with the guidelines was checked using a ROM during development, the compliance results might change as a result of subsequent development.
Be sure to check again as needed so the final master ROM complies with the guidelines.


Info:

By setting the feature to receive update notifications, you can avoid missing out on information about updates to the guidelines.

Several pages on NDP have a feature for sending update information by email.
The following page lists updates to technical information, including the guidelines.

    • Nintendo Developer Portal > Developer Support > Nintendo Switch > Technical Information

As shown below, if you click Subscribe on this page, the wording changes to display Unsubscribe.
In this state, you are subscribed and will receive email notifications of the update information on this page.

Submitting a Waiver Request

You can submit a waiver request only when you have exhausted all other options and still cannot comply with a guideline due to special circumstances.

Related Documents
  • 7 Waiver Requests > Waiver Request Process.
  • Nintendo Switch Master ROM Submission Procedures > Submitting a Master ROM > Submitting Waiver Requests
Related NDP Pages
  • NDP > MY PRODUCTS > target_product > Releases or Downloadable Content > target_release > go to the guide on the right side of the screen > Guidelines
  • NDP > MY PRODUCTS > target_product > Releases or Downloadable Content > target_release > go to the guide on the right side of the screen > Issues
Procedure
  1. Select the check box for the guideline you cannot conform to.



  2. Complete step 4 in the preceding section, Checking Guideline Compliance, and save the reviewed content.
  3. In the list of tasks on the guide on the right side of the screen, click Issues. Submit a waiver request from the displayed Required Approvals (n) tab (where n indicates the number of required approvals).
    It may take time to discuss the content of a waiver request, so contact the NDP Licensing contact as far in advance as possible. (Reference: 9 Contacts for Inquiries Before, During, and After Lotcheck Testing)
