4.6 Considering a Precheck

Before beginning official Lotcheck, software may be subject to limited testing within an area specified by the developer, or receive a precheck restricted to only guideline testing.

Use the results of this testing to prepare efficiently for the official Lotcheck. However, precheck is performed for ROMs that are still in development, and sufficient testing may not be possible depending on how complete the ROM is.
Also, there is no pass or fail judgment for precheck.

If you would like to undergo precheck, contact Nintendo in advance to see whether it is possible. It is possible that we may not support this option due to the circumstances or effects.
When contacting Nintendo, include information such as your application's specifications and development status.

How to Contact Nintendo if You Would Like to Undergo Precheck

If you would like to undergo precheck, use either email or the Messages tab on the Product Dashboard to contact Nintendo.

Reference: 9 Contacts for Inquiries Before, During, and After Lotcheck Testing

How to Request Precheck

If, as a result of contacting Nintendo, Nintendo agrees to perform precheck, please follow the steps below to request precheck.

Related NDP Pages

NDP > MY PRODUCTS > target_product > Releases or Downloadable Content > target_release > go to the guide on the right side of the screen > Release Info

Procedure

1. Click Release Info in the guide on the right side of the screen.

2. Select Precheck for Submission Type and request Lotcheck.
