6 Checking Lotcheck Progress

After submitting to Lotcheck, you can verify the Lotcheck progress from the Releases tab for each product.

Associated NDP Pages

NDP > MY PRODUCTS > target_product > Releases tab


Note: The status is also displayed on the Release Info page on the Release Dashboard.

Status Changes Before and After Submitting to Lotcheck

NDP Status (Japanese) NDP Status (English) Situation Notes

開発中

In Development

 Has not yet been submitted to Lotcheck. -

ロットチェックへ提出中

Submitting to Lotcheck

 The software has been submitted to Lotcheck and the ROM submission is being processed. The ROM is being checked for errors. The status of OMAS submissions is also checked at the same time. The process is suspended if inconsistencies with the NDP information are detected.
In this case, see Important! Statuses Where Problems May Occur later on this page.

ロットチェックへ提出済み

Submitted to Lotcheck

 The ROM was submitted without any issues and is waiting for the Lotcheck department to accept it. -

検査開始待ち

In Queue

 The ROM was accepted by the Lotcheck department without any issues and is awaiting testing.

After issues for the information entered in Features, Test Scenarios, and Age Rating are fixed by the developer and they click the Submit Edited Information button, the status reverts to this status from In Testing.
If the Lotcheck department then confirms the fixed content, the status transitions to In Testing again.

検査中

In Testing

 Testing has started.

This status is displayed until the Lotcheck Issues in NDP have been handled by the developer, even after testing is complete. After handling, the status transitions to the judgment status based on the content of the issues.

ロットチェック合格

Passed Lotcheck

 The software has passed Lotcheck. -

キャンセル処理待ち

Pending Cancellation

The developer has clicked the Cancel Submission button, and Nintendo is verifying the situation.
Note: The submission is not canceled immediately when the button is clicked, but may require some time.

If the Cancel Submission button is clicked when the submission version is in Submitted to Lotcheck status, the version is canceled immediately.
When the status is In Queue or In Testing, the submission is not canceled immediately, and this status is displayed.

Statuses Where Problems May Occur

Note:

If one of the following statuses is displayed, an NDP error may have occurred or handling may have been omitted.
Contact NDP licensing if none of the following apply and the issue cannot be resolved. (Reference: 9 Contacts for Inquiries Before, During, and After Lotcheck Testing)

Submitting to Lotcheck

In Testing

If the status remains unchanged for six business days or more, it may be under consideration in a thread on the developer side.
An issue was reported by the Lotcheck Department in Lotcheck Issues, but the developer did not notice it and did not respond to the thread or handle the issue.

Check the Issues section on the Release Dashboard.

Statuses not described on this page

The Lotcheck submission processing may have failed on NDP.

Contact NDP Licensing immediately.
