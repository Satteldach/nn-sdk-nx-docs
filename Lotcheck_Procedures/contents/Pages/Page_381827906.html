4.5 Resolving Issues

The Issues page displays specific action requests for the developer.
They are listed dynamically, depending on your actions on other pages of the release dashboard.

Related NDP Pages

NDP > MY PRODUCTS > target_product > Releases or Downloadable Content > target_release > go to the guide on the right side of the screen > Issues

Procedure

1. Enter the prescribed information in the other pages on the release dashboard and then upload the master ROM.

Note: The other pages are Release Info, Features, Guidelines, Age Rating, and ROM Upload.

2. Each time an operation is performed in step 1, NDP checks whether there is a condition that requires other action in the entered content or the master ROM.
　If anything is applicable, it is listed as an issue on this page.

3. Follow the instructions on the screen to resolve each issue that is listed. Resolution methods are also displayed.

Note: Contact Nintendo if you do not know how to resolve an issue. (Reference: 9 Contacts for Inquiries Before, During, and After Lotcheck Testing)

4. When the listed issues are all resolved, a check mark will appear in Issues in the guide on the right side of the screen, indicating that the task has been completed.

Info:

Even if there is a check mark in Issues in the guide on the right side of the screen, subsequent actions might increase the number of issues, in which case the check mark indicating completion will be removed.

About Each Tab

This section describes what kind of content is listed in each tab.

Lotcheck Issues

Info:

If you are submitting the product to Lotcheck for the first time, there is no information displayed in this tab before Lotcheck submission.

  • Lotcheck issues reported in past submissions

The preceding issues are listed before submitting to Lotcheck.
These are threads that have not been closed for Lotcheck issues that were reported in past submissions of the same product.

Expand the Unresolved category and check them.
Click Details to display the procedure for closing the issue. Follow that procedure to deal with the issue.

Required Approvals

  • Specifications on the Features page that are marked as requiring Nintendo approval.
  • Items marked as unable to comply with the guidelines on the Guidelines page.
  • Information detected as requiring Nintendo approval in the master ROM uploaded from the ROM Upload page

Information about these operations are listed prior to Lotcheck submission. You must submit waiver requests one at a time.
For more information about handling these issues, see 7 Waiver Requests > What Are Waiver Requests? > Two Types of Timing for Waiver Requests > Before Submitting to Lotcheck.

Release Errors

  • Issues in the ROM or entries in the release dashboard that require revision.
