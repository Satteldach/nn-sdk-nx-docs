5 Items for the Developer to Address During Lotcheck

Even after submitting the master ROM to Lotcheck, there are still items on NDP that require developer operation.

During the Lotcheck process, Nintendo contacts the developer to ask questions, report problems with the information entered in NDP, and report issues as needed. This information is required in order to progress with Lotcheck and make judgments, so please respond as soon as possible. Aim to respond or send handling methods within two business days after a report is posted.

Lotcheck is paused until the issues are handled by the developer.

For example, if you respond the day a report is made, work will resume the next business day. However, if a response is made two business days after the report is made, work will resume three days after the report.
We ask for your cooperation so that release schedules are not affected.

Handling Issues Reported by the Lotcheck Department

If any issues are found during Lotcheck, Nintendo reports them to the developer as they occur in the Issues > Lotcheck Issues tab on NDP.

This report requires a response from the developer to progress with Lotcheck and issue judgment.

The following table summarizes the handling methods based on the type of report.

Note:

If there are any misunderstandings in the report from the Lotcheck Department, add a comment in the corresponding thread to point out the misunderstandings.

When you do, also provide corrections and describe the reasons, if applicable. In this case, do not select a Resolution.

Note:

If the developer does not respond to a report from Lotcheck within a certain period, Nintendo contacts the developer to prompt a response.

If the developer still does not respond, Nintendo halts the Lotcheck procedure and the release is processed as having failed Lotcheck.
If the happens, Lotcheck can be resumed by making a request to Nintendo.

A report that requires revising Features, Test Scenarios, or Age Rating.

This report occurs when behavior that differs from information entered in NDP is confirmed during testing.

  2. Post questions to the thread as needed.
  3. If what is reported is correct, in the release dashboard go to Issues > Lotcheck Issues, and for Resolution select Will Fix in Features for the corresponding item.

What to Do When a Bug Is Discovered by the Developer After Uploading the Master ROM

If the developer discovers a bug after uploading the master ROM to NDP, the handling procedure is dependent on the timing.

This may affect your release schedule, so refer to each of the following cases and cancel your submission according to the guidance you receive from your NDP Licensing contact.

(Reference: 9 Contacts for Inquiries Before, During, and After Lotcheck Testing)

Case 1: A bug was found before submitting to Lotcheck.

  1. Fix the bug and upload the master ROM again.

  2. If fixing the bug takes time and the ROM cannot be submitted in accordance to the schedule that was reported in advance, consult with NDP Licensing.
    This may affect the release date or production schedule.

Case 2: A bug was discovered by the developer after submitting to Lotcheck but before Lotcheck judgment.

It can take several days for the Lotcheck Department to complete the cancellation process after the developer has canceled their submission.
The revised master ROM can only be uploaded after the cancellation process is completed.

The Lotcheck Department performs some tests before completing the cancellation process. They will report any bugs they find.

If there is no leeway in your release schedule, contact your NDP Licensing contact about the bug situation and your ROM resubmission schedule before you cancel your submission.
When doing so, developers who are advised by NCL personnel need to contact NDP Licensing; developers who are advised by NOA or NOE personnel need to contact their respective Lotcheck department.


  1. Click the Cancel Submission button on NDP.



  2. To check on the completion of the cancellation process, check the release status or check for a notification email.
    After the cancellation processing completes, upload the fixed master ROM again.

    Supplement: If the status becomes Pending Cancellation, cancellation has not completed because Nintendo is verifying the situation.
    After cancellation processing completes, the release status changes to In Development, and the submitted version increments.
    (For more information, see 6 Checking Lotcheck Progress > Status Changes Before and After Submitting to Lotcheck.)

Case 3: A bug was found after passing Lotcheck but before release.

The ROM for release cannot be modified after you have received notification that it has passed Lotcheck. Any subsequent bug fixes must be released as patches.
Contact NDP Licensing if there are unavoidable circumstances.
