4.1 Entering Release Information

Nintendo performs Lotcheck based on the information the developer enters in NDP. This page describes how to enter information for the application on the Release Info page, and provides related information.

If there are any errors in the content that is entered, Nintendo may contact the developer, which increases the Lotcheck time. Check that the information entered is correct before submitting to Lotcheck.

Associated NDP Pages

NDP > MY PRODUCTS > target_product > Releases or Downloadable Content > target_release > > go to the guide on the right side of the screen > Release Info

Procedure
  1. Click Release Info.

  2. Enter the required options, and then click the Save button at the bottom of the page. You can also go back and edit the entries again.

    Info:

    Input data can be copied from another release. As shown in the red box in the following image, click Copy Release Data. Follow the instructions on the screen.
