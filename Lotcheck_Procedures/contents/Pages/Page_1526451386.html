4.3 Testing ROM Upload

Try uploading a provisional ROM at the development stage when all of the specifications have been implemented on the ROM.

When you use the ROM Upload feature on NDP, the ROM is checked for errors based on the release information entered in NDP and the information on the NMETA file recorded in the ROM.
By conducting a test upload before you upload the finished master ROM, you can spot areas that need fixing at an earlier stage.

Info:
  • For more information about the upload procedure, see Nintendo Switch Master ROM Submission Procedures > Submitting a Master ROM > Uploading Master ROMs.
  • To read the FAQ about these operations on NDP, see Help > My Products > Releases > ROM Upload.
Related NDP Pages

NDP > My Products > target_product > Releases or Downloadable Content > target_release > go to the guide on the right side of the screen > ROM Upload

Procedure
  1. Follow the directions presented on the screen and perform the upload.
  2. If the system detects issues that require fixing, the issues will be listed in Issues in the Required Approvals tab or the Release Errors tab.
    Use this information to make revisions to the ROM.
    If the ROM is fine but you entered incorrect information on any page of the Release Dashboard, make corrections to the information you entered.
  3. If after ROM upload the system detects a function that requires special handling, it automatically adds items to Guidelines or Test Scenarios.
    Be sure to handle these newly added items.
