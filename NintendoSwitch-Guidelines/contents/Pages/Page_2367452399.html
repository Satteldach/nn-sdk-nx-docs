Contacting Nintendo about your application's specifications

Your application's specifications determine which guidelines it must follow. You are required to inform Nintendo about your application's specifications by filling out the Application Specifications forms on Nintendo Developer Portal. Follow the steps below to access the forms where you fill out your application specifications.

  1. On Nintendo Developer Portal, open the My Products page.
  2. Select the Product for the application you are working on.
  3. Select the Release for the master ROM you are submitting.
  4. Select Features.
