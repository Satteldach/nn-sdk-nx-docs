3.3 (EU)Health and Safety Information

Please read and observe the health and safety information. Failure to do so could result in injury or damage to the SDEV kit.

WARNING – Seizures

Some people (about 1 in 4000) may have seizures or blackouts triggered by light flashes or patterns, and this may occur while they are watching TV or playing video games, even if they have never had a seizure before. Anyone who has ever experienced seizures, loss of awareness or any other symptom linked to an epileptic condition should consult a doctor before using this device.

Stop using this device and consult a doctor if you have any unusual symptoms, such as: convulsions, eye or muscle twitching, loss of awareness, altered vision, involuntary movements, or disorientation.

To reduce the likelihood of a seizure when using this device:

  • Do not use this device if you are tired or need sleep.
  • Use this device in a well-lit room.
  • Take a break of 10 to 15 minutes every hour.

WARNING – Eye Strain, Motion Sickness and Repetitive Motion Injuries

Do not use this device for excessively long periods of time in order to avoid eye strain, motion sickness and repetitive motion injuries.

Take a break of 10 to 15 minutes every hour, even if you don’t think you need it.

Stop using this device if you experience any of these symptoms:

  • If your eyes become tired or sore while playing, or if you feel dizzy, nauseated or tired;
  • If your hands, wrists, or arms become tired or sore while playing, or if you feel tingling, numbness, burning or stiffness or other discomfort.

If any of these symptoms persist, consult a doctor.

WARNING – Pregnancy and Medical Conditions

Consult a doctor before using this device if you will be engaging in physical activity when using it and:

  • You are pregnant;
  • You suffer from heart, respiratory, back, joint or orthopaedic problems;
  • You have high blood pressure;
  • Your doctor has instructed you to restrict your physical activity;
  • You have any other medical condition that may be aggravated by physical activity.

Persons who have an injury or disorder involving their fingers, hands or arms should not use the vibration feature.

WARNING - Pacemaker

Do not use this device within 15 centimetres of a cardiac pacemaker while using wireless communication.

If you have a pacemaker or other implanted medical device, consult a doctor before using the SDEV kit.

WARNING – Batteries

The SDEV kit contains a lithium coin cell battery (CR2032). If the battery is swallowed, it can cause severe internal burns in just two hours and can lead to death.

  • Do not ingest the battery: Chemical Burn Hazard.
  • The battery is not rechargeable. Do not attempt to recharge.
  • When the battery is flat, remove it from the SDEV kit.
  • Keep new and used batteries away from children. If the battery compartment does not close securely, stop using the SDEV kit and keep it away from children. If you think batteries might have been swallowed or put inside any part of the body, seek immediate medical attention.
  • Do not short-circuit the battery or the terminals in the battery compartment.
  • There is a risk of battery explosion if an incorrect battery type is used.

WARNING - Electrical Safety

Observe the following precautions to reduce the risk of fire or shock.

When using the AC adapter:

  • Use only the Nintendo AC adapter (HAC-002) to charge the console.
  • Connect the AC adapter to the correct voltage (AC 100 – 240V).
  • Do not use the AC adapter if it has damaged, split, or broken cords or wires.
  • Do not use voltage transformers or plugs that deliver reduced amounts of electricity.
  • The AC adapter should be fully inserted into a nearby, easily accessible socket.
  • Always carefully disconnect all plugs by pulling on the plug and not on the cord.
  • The AC adapter is for indoor use only.
  • If you hear a strange noise, see smoke or smell something strange, unplug the AC adapter from the socket and contact Ordering_Publisher@nintendo.de for assistance.

Do not expose the SDEV kit to fire, microwaves, direct sunlight, high or extremely low temperatures. 

Do not let the SDEV kit come into contact with liquid and do not use it with wet or oily hands.
If liquid gets inside, stop using and contact Ordering_Publisher@nintendo.de for assistance.

Do not apply excessive force to this device.

Do not pull on cables and do not twist them too tightly.

Do not touch device connectors with your fingers or metal objects.

Do not touch the AC adapter or connected devices while charging during a thunderstorm.

Use only compatible accessories that have been approved for use in your country.

Do not disassemble or try to repair this device.
If any parts of the SDEV kit are damaged, stop using them and contact Ordering_Publisher@nintendo.de for assistance. Do not touch damaged areas. Avoid contact with any leaking fluid.

WARNING - Heat

Stop playing if you are holding the console or the controllers while charging and they become too hot, as this may lead to skin burns.

Avoid prolonged contact with parts warmer than body temperature.
Some development hardware products become hot and pose a risk of low-temperature burns.
See a medical professional immediately if you have suffered a low-temperature burn.
Cooling the area with water is ineffective on a low-temperature burn.

WARNING - General

Keep this device, its accessories and packaging materials away from young children and pets.
Small parts such as game cards, microSD cards and packaging items may be accidentally ingested. The cables can coil around the neck.

Wireless communication may not be allowed in certain places such as aeroplanes or hospitals.
Please follow respective regulations.

Do not use excessive volume for headphones.

Do not use headphones to listen at high volume levels for long periods due to high sound pressure and hearing damage risk. Keep the volume at a level at which you can hear your surroundings. Consult a doctor if you experience symptoms such as buzzing in your ears.

CAREFUL USAGE

Do not place the console in humid areas or areas where the temperature can suddenly change.
If condensation forms, turn the power off and wait until the water droplets have evaporated.

Do not use in dusty or smoky areas.

Do not cover the console's air intakes or vents to avoid overheating the device.

If  any parts of the SDEV kit become dirty, wipe them clean with a soft, dry cloth.
Avoid using alcohol, thinner or other solvents.

Be aware of your surroundings while using this device.

Make sure to charge the built-in batteries at least once every six months.
If the batteries are not used for an extended period of time, it may become impossible to charge them.
