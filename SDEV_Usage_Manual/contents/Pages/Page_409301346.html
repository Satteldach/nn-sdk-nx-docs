6.2.3 Connecting USB Devices

Connect a USB device to the SDEV using one of the following procedures.

  • Connect the USB device through the SDEV cradle.
  • Connect a USB device directly to the SDEV console.
Connecting a USB Device Through the SDEV Cradle

When connecting a USB device through the SDEV cradle, use the following procedure and connect the SDEV console to the SDEV cradle before turning on the SDEV cradle.

Note

To avoid malfunction or damage, use only the SDEV cradle that came with the system.

  1. Turn the SDEV off.
    Unplug the Wii U AC adapter from the SDEV console.
  2. Unplug the AC adapter for the SDEV cradle from the power outlet.
  3. Unplug the AC adapter from the SDEV cradle.
  4. Connect the SDEV cradle to the USB Type-C port on the bottom of the SDEV console.
  5. Connect the USB device to the USB port on the front of the SDEV cradle.
  6. Connect the AC adapter to the AC adapter connector on the front of the SDEV cradle.

  7. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.
    The SDEV cradle turns on, and the TV output LED lights up temporarily.

    Info

    Use the dedicated AC adapter for Switch (the HAC-002).

    If the TV output LED on the SDEV cradle does not light up, see 9.4 SDEV Cradle Does Not Turn On.

  8. Turn the SDEV on.
    Connect the Wii U AC adapter to the SDEV console.

    Info

    If the USB device still cannot be detected after performing these operations, see 9.10 Cannot Detect USB Devices.

Connecting the USB Device Directly to the SDEV Console

When the SDEV is on, when you connect a USB device to the USB Type-C port on the bottom of the SDEV console, the USB device is recognized.

However, as with the Switch retail product, operation is not assured for devices connected to the SDEV console.
