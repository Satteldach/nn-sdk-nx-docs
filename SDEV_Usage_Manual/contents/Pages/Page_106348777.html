5.1 Turning the SDEV On and Off

This section describes how to turn the SDEV on and off.

Use the Wii U AC adapter to turn the SDEV on and off.

Info

You cannot turn the SDEV on and off by pressing the POWER Button on the top of the SDEV console.

Turning the SDEV On

  1. Connect the Wii U AC adapter to the AC adapter connector on the top of the SDEV console.

    Note

    Even if you connect the AC adapter (HAC-002) to the SDEV console, the SDEV does not turn on. Always connect the Wii U AC adapter to the SDEV.

    The SDEV turns on, and the POWER LED lights up.

    Note

    The SDEV console does not come with a battery, so it cannot be charged. When using the SDEV, always have it connected to the Wii U AC adapter.

    By default, turning the SDEV on does not display anything on the screen. For more information about setting up the SDEV, see the Nintendo Switch SDEV Quick Start.


Turning the SDEV Off
  1. Press and hold down the POWER Button on the top of the SDEV for at least 7 seconds.
  2. Unplug the Wii U AC adapter from the SDEV console. The SDEV turns off, and the power LED turns off.
