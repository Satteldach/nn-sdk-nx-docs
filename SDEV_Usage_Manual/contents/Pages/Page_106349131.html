8.4 Switching Between Handheld and TV Modes

You can switch between handheld mode and TV mode by simply connecting and disconnecting the SDEV console and the SDEV cradle. Switching between handheld mode and TV mode changes the audio and video output destination in addition to affecting the performance.

Info

Audio and video output can only be sent to a TV or LCD screen.

Note

To avoid malfunction or damage, use only the SDEV cradle that came with the system.

Switching to TV Mode

Connect the SDEV cradle to the SDEV to switch to TV mode.

When switching to TV mode, check the following.

  • Is the AC adapter properly connected to the SDEV cradle?
  • Is the SDEV cradle properly connected to the TV with an HDMI cable?
Switching to Handheld Mode

Remove the SDEV cradle from the SDEV to switch to handheld mode.
