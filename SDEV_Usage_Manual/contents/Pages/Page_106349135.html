8.5 Mirroring SDEV Audio and Video to a TV

You can mirror the audio and video on the SDEV to a TV, regardless of whether the SDEV system is in handheld mode or TV mode.

During mirroring, audio is not switched to play in TV mode. Instead, the audio is played through the TV speakers at the same level as the SDEV audio.

Info

Video output from the HDMI port on the SDEV console is always in full-range RGB, regardless of the SDEV settings. For this reason, depending on the TV you are using, the RGB range may be misidentified and not display correctly. When checking the output from the HDMI port on the SDEV console, we recommend configuring the RGB range on the TV to full range.

Use the following procedure to mirror the SDEV audio and video on a TV.

  1. Connect an HDMI cable to the HDMI output port on the top of the SDEV console.
  2. Connect the HDMI cable to a TV.
