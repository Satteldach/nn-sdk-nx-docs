5.3 Turning the SDEV Cradle On and Off

This section describes how to turn the SDEV cradle on and off.

Use the AC adapter to turn the SDEV cradle on and off.

Note

To avoid malfunction or damage, use only the SDEV cradle that came with the system.

Turning the SDEV Cradle On

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the AC adapter connector on the front of the SDEV cradle.

  2. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The SDEV cradle turns on, and the TV output LED lights up temporarily.

    Info

    Use the dedicated AC adapter for Switch (the HAC-002).

    If the TV output LED on the SDEV cradle does not light up, see 9.4 SDEV Cradle Does Not Turn On.

Turning the SDEV Cradle Off

  1. Unplug the AC adapter from the power outlet.

    The SDEV cradle turns off.
