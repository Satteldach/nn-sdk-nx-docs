8.2 Initializing the SDEV

Update the SDEV firmware and initialize the SDEV.
When you initialize the SDEV, the installed applications and settings are reset.

For more information about initializing the SDEV, see Nintendo Switch SDEV Quick Start > Initializing the SDEV.

Note

To avoid malfunction, do not turn off the power to the SDEV or remove game cards or microSD cards during SDEV initialization. If the SDEV malfunctions, it cannot be restored and it must be repaired at Nintendo.
