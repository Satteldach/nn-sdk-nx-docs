9.8 No TV Video and Audio Output

If there is no TV video or audio output, check the following.

Connected to the TV Via the SDEV Cradle
  • Is the HDMI cable correctly connected to the SDEV cradle?
  • Are the TV input settings correct?
  • IS the SDEV console correctly connected to the SDEV cradle?
  • Is the TV output LED on the SDEV cradle flashing?
    If it is flashing, see 9.5 Flashing LED on the SDEV Cradle.

If you do not find any problems while checking these things, perform the following operations.

  1. Remove the SDEV from the SDEV cradle.
  2. Restart the SDEV if no image appears on the SDEV LCD screen.
    For more information about restarting the SDEV, see 8.1 Restarting the SDEV.
  3. After making sure that an image appears on the SDEV LCD screen, connect the SDEV to the SDEV cradle.
Connected to the TV From the HDMI Jack on the SDEV
  • Is the HDMI cable properly connected to the SDEV?
  • Are the TV input settings correct?

If you do not find any problems while checking these things, perform the following operations.

  1. Unplug and then reconnect the HDMI cable while video output is being sent to the SDEV screen.
  2. If there is still no video output, restart the SDEV.
    For more information about restarting the SDEV, see 8.1 Restarting the SDEV.
  3. Unplug and reconnect the HDMI cable again.
