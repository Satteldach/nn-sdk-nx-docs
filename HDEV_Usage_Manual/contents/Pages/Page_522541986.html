9.1 HDEV Does Not Turn On

The HDEV might have stopped responding. Press and hold the POWER Button on the top of the HDEV for at least 12 seconds to turn the HDEV off, and then press the POWER Button again.

If the preceding operations do not turn the power on, the battery might not be sufficiently charged. Charge the HDEV as described in 5.1 Charging the HDEV.

If charging the HDEV does not resolve the issue, perform the following checks.

Check the AC adapter you are using.

Use the dedicated AC adapter for Switch (the HAC-002).

Check that you are using a correct method of charging.

Check that the AC adapter is properly connected to the HDEV.
Check that the AC adapter is plugged into the power outlet correctly.

If there were no problems with these connections, disconnect the AC adapter from the power outlet, wait 10 seconds, and then reconnect it.
