6.2.3 Connecting USB Devices

Connect a USB device to the HDEV using the following procedure.

  • Directly connect a USB device to the HDEV console.
Directly Connecting a USB Device to the HDEV Console

When you connect a USB device to the USB Type-C port on the bottom of the HDEV console, the USB device is recognized regardless of whether the HDEV is turned on.

However, like with the Switch Lite retail product, operation is not assured for devices connected to the HDEV.
