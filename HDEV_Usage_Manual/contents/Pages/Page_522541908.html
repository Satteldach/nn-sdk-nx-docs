6.1 Connecting the HDEV and a PC

To perform tasks like installing applications and writing to NX development cards, the HDEV must be connected to a PC.

The relay box is used to connect the HDEV to a PC.

Note

The relay box cannot be used to connect a USB device even when it is not connected to a PC.

The image shows the setup when the HDEV is connected to a TV.

To create this setup, use the following procedure to connect the HDEV to a PC.

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the relay box.
    Connect the AC adapter to the AC adapter connector on the front of the relay box.

  2. Connect the relay box to the PC.
    Connect the USB Micro-B to Micro-A cable to the USB port on the front of the relay box and then connect it to the PC.

  3. Connect the relay box to the HDEV.
    Connect the relay box to the USB Type-C connector on the bottom of the HDEV.
