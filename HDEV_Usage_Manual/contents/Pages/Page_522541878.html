5.1 Charging the HDEV

To charge the HDEV, use any of the following methods.

  • Connect the AC adapter to the HDEV to charge.
  • Connect the relay box to the HDEV to charge.
Note

The HDEV does not charge if it is simply connected to a PC via a USB cable.

Connecting the AC Adapter to the HDEV to Charge
  1. Connect the AC adapter to the USB Type-C connector on the bottom of the HDEV.
  2. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The HDEV starts charging.

Connecting the Relay Box to the HDEV to Charge

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the AC adapter connector on the front of the relay box.

  2. Connect the relay box to the USB Type-C connector on the bottom of the HDEV.

  3. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The HDEV starts charging.
