8.2 Initializing the HDEV

Update the HDEV firmware and initialize the HDEV.
When you initialize the HDEV, the installed applications and settings are reset.

Before you initialize the HDEV, follow the procedures in 5.1 Charging the HDEV to make sure that the device is sufficiently charged.

For more information about initializing the HDEV, see Nintendo Switch HDEV Quick Start > Initializing the HDEV.

Note

To avoid malfunction, do not turn off the power to the HDEV or remove game cards or microSD cards during HDEV initialization. If the HDEV malfunctions, it cannot be restored and it must be repaired at Nintendo.
