2.3 Restrictions on Flashing Saturated Red Colors

Do not use a sequence of images that does all of the following.

  1. Includes flashing. (Regardless of the change in luminance of the flashes.)
  2. One of the images contains saturated red.
  3. The saturated red occupies more than one-eighth of the screen.
  4. Has more than three flashes occurring in any 1-second period.
Info

The term saturated red refers to colors where the R component is greater than 85% of the sum of the color's RGB values.

You can determine the R value with the following equation.
Rlinear ≥ 0.85 x (Rlinear + Glinear + Blinear)

These RGB values are the RGB values of the TV screen, indicating a linear color space.

Info

Nintendo Switch has a TV mode and a handheld mode. For TV mode, the basic rule is to evaluate the luminance of your application based on the assumption that your application is displayed on the TV screen.

If your application has significantly different effects or appearance in TV mode versus handheld mode, you must evaluate your application separately for each mode.
