Each folder contains a bmp folder, an ai folder, and an excel folder.

  • The XXX portion of these filenames is the supported language.
    • ja: Japanese / en: English (both US English and UK English) / en-US: US English / en-EU: British English / de: German / fr: French / es: Spanish / it: Italian / nl: Dutch / pt: European Portuguese / pt-BR: Brazilian Portuguese / ru: Russian / ko: Korean / zh-CN: Chinese (Simplified ) / zh-TW: Chinese (Traditional)
  • The YYY portion of these filenames is the resolution (720p, 1080p, or 4K).
  • The VER portion of these filenames is the version.
  • The REGION portion of these filenames is the region.
    • asia: For Asia / useu: For Europe and North America
  • The AI files are in RGB format.
  • You need Adobe Illustrator CS6 or later to open these AI files.
