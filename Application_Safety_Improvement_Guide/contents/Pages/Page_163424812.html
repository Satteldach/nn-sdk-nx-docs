2.5 Restrictions on Regular Patterns

Do not use images that do all of the following.

  1. Consists of striped patterns composed of parallel lines or dots or other regular elements with distinct edges, such as the examples shown in Figure 2-1 Regular Patterns.
  2. Has high contrast between the bright and dark elements of the pattern.
  3. Occupies more than one-fourth of the screen.
  4. Has more than five light–dark pairs of stripes in any orientation.


Figure 2-1 Regular Patterns

Info

The stripes may be parallel or radial, curved or straight, black and white, or a combination of colors. Avoid especially stripes that oscillate or flash and moving stripes that change direction. Do not switch the luminance of the lighter and darker stripes (so that the dark becomes light and vice versa). However, striped patterns that flow smoothly across, into, or out of the screen in one direction may be used. Checkerboard patterns and plaids are also acceptable.

Info

Nintendo Switch has a TV mode and a handheld mode. For TV mode, the basic rule is to evaluate the luminance of your application based on the assumption that your application is displayed on the TV screen.

If your application has significantly different effects or appearance in TV mode versus handheld mode, you must evaluate your application separately for each mode.
