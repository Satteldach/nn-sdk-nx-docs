5.1 Examples of Application Designs That Take Safety Into Account

We recommend that any application that uses the vibration feature include measures to promote a fun and safe experience. These measures can be implemented in various ways, including the application's settings and rules. See the following examples.

Avoid continuous vibration

To the extent possible, avoid continuously vibrating the linear resonant actuator.
It is undesirable from both the health and safety standpoint and the product lifetime standpoint to have any situation where the linear resonant actuator vibrates indefinitely when a button is pressed.

Take precautions when using vibration semi-continuously for long periods of time

Being subjected to strong vibration for long periods of time may place strain on the user's wrists and may cause discomfort.

Consider ensuring that there are periods without vibration, for example by putting a no-vibration scene in the transition between other scenes.
