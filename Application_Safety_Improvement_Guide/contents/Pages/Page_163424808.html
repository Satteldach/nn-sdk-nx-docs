2.4 Restrictions on Image Reversals

Do not use a sequence of images that does all of the following.

  1. Switches or interchanges the luminance of the elements of an image that occupies more than one-fourth of the screen. (For example, switching an image between positive and negative or between black and white, as shown in Figure 2-1 Changes in Screen Contrast and Luminance.)
  2. Exhibits changes in luminance of the image elements that exceed 20 cd/m².
  3. Has more than three changes in luminance occurring in any 1-second period.
Figure 2-1 Changes in Screen Contrast and Luminance

Info

Nintendo Switch has a TV mode and a handheld mode. For TV mode, the basic rule is to evaluate the luminance of your application based on the assumption that your application is displayed on the TV screen.

If your application has significantly different effects or appearance in TV mode versus handheld mode, you must evaluate your application separately for each mode.
