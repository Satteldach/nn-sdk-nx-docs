5.1 Charging the ADEV

To charge the ADEV, use any of the following methods.

  • Connect the AC adapter to the ADEV and recharge.
  • Connect the Relay Box-A to the ADEV and recharge.
  • Attach the ADEV to the Nintendo Switch dock and recharge.
Note

The ADEV does not recharge if it is simply connected to a PC via a USB cable.

Connecting the AC Adapter to the ADEV and Recharging
  1. Connect the AC adapter to the USB Type-C connector on the bottom of the ADEV.
  2. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The ADEV starts charging.

Connecting the Relay Box-A to the ADEV and Recharging

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the AC adapter connector on the front of the Relay Box-A.

  2. Connect the Relay Box-A to the USB Type-C terminal on the lower part of the ADEV.

  3. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The ADEV starts charging.

Attaching the ADEV to the Nintendo Switch Dock and Recharging

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the Nintendo Switch dock.
    Open the cover on the back of the Nintendo Switch dock and connect the AC adapter to the top connector.

  2. Attach the ADEV to the Nintendo Switch dock.

  3. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The TV output LED illuminates temporarily and the ADEV starts charging.

    Info

    If the TV output LED on the Nintendo Switch dock does not illuminate, see 9.5 Nintendo Switch Dock Does Not Turn On.
