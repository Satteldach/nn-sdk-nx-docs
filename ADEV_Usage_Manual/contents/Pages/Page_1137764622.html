6.1 Connecting the ADEV and a PC

The ADEV may need to be connected to a PC when installing applications or writing to an NX development card.

The Relay Box-A is used to connect the ADEV to a PC.

The following actions are not supported while the ADEV is connected to the Relay Box-A because it is normally in TV mode.

  • Video output to the ADEV screen
  • Operation of a Joy-Con attached to the ADEV

To operate the ADEV while it is connected to a PC, connect the ADEV to a TV and use a detached Joy-Con, or switch the ADEV to handheld mode.

For more information about switching to handheld mode, see 8.4 Switching Between Handheld and TV Modes.

Note

If the ADEV is attached to the Nintendo Switch dock and the dock is connected to the Relay Box-A, the PC cannot recognize the ADEV. Remove the ADEV from the Nintendo Switch dock and connect it directly to the Relay Box-A.

You cannot use connected USB devices regardless of whether the Relay Box-A is connected to a PC.

The image shows the setup when the ADEV is connected to both a PC and a TV.

To create this setup, use the following procedure to connect the ADEV to a PC.

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the Relay Box-A.
    Connect the AC adapter to the AC adapter connector on the front of the Relay Box-A.

  2. Connect the Relay Box-A to the PC.
    Connect the USB Micro-B to Micro-A cable to the USB port on the front of the Relay Box-A and then connect it to the PC.

  3. Connect the Relay Box-A to the TV.
    Connect the HDMI cable to the HDMI output port on the front of the Relay Box-A and then connect it to the TV.

  4. Connect the Relay Box-A to the ADEV.
    Connect the Relay Box-A to the USB Type-C terminal on the lower part of the ADEV.
