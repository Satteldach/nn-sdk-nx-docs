5.2 Turning the ADEV On and Off

This section describes how to turn the ADEV on and off.

Turning the ADEV On

  1. Press the POWER Button on the top of the ADEV.

    The ADEV turns on.

    Note

    If you are using the ADEV for the first time, see the Nintendo Switch ADEV Quick Start to set up the device before reading this documentation.

Turning the ADEV Off

  1. Press and hold down the POWER Button on the top of the ADEV for at least 7 seconds.

    The ADEV turns off.
