6.2.3 Connecting USB Devices

Connect a USB device to the ADEV using one of the following procedures.

  • Connect the USB device via the Nintendo Switch dock
  • Connect the USB device directly to the ADEV
Connecting the USB Device via the Nintendo Switch Dock

To connect a USB device to the ADEV via the Nintendo Switch dock, use the following procedure to connect the ADEV to the dock before supplying power to the dock.

  1. Turn the ADEV off.
    Press and hold down the POWER Button on the top of the ADEV for at least 7 seconds.

  2. Attach the ADEV to the Nintendo Switch dock.

  3. Connect the USB device to the USB terminal on the Nintendo Switch dock.

  4. Connect the AC adapter to the Nintendo Switch dock.
    Connect the AC adapter to the uppermost connector.


    The power turns on to the Nintendo Switch dock and the TV output LED illuminates briefly.

    Info

    If the TV output LED on the Nintendo Switch dock does not illuminate, see 9.5 Nintendo Switch Dock Does Not Turn On.

  5. Press the POWER Button on the top of the ADEV.

    The ADEV turns on.

Connecting the USB Device Directly to the ADEV Console

When you connect a USB device to the USB Type-C port on the bottom of the ADEV console, the USB device is recognized regardless of whether the ADEV is on.

However, as with the Switch retail product, operation is not assured for devices connected to the ADEV console.
