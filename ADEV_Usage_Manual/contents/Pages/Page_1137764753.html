9.6 Flashing LED on the Nintendo Switch Dock

If the TV output LED on the Nintendo Switch dock is flashing, check for problems in the connection between the Nintendo Switch dock and the ADEV.

If there is no problem with the connection, complete the following operations.

  1. Remove the ADEV from the Nintendo Switch dock.
  2. Restart the ADEV.
    For more information about restarting the ADEV, see 8.1 Restarting the ADEV.
  3. Unplug the AC adapter from the Nintendo Switch dock.
  4. Connect the AC adapter to the Nintendo Switch dock.
  5. Attach the ADEV to the Nintendo Switch dock.
