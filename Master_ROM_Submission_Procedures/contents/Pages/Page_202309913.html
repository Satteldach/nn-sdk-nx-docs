3 Overall Process for Submitting a Master ROM

This section describes the overall process when submitting the master ROM for an application.

A more detailed representation of the process is presented in Overall Process for Submitting a Master ROM (Details), located below Figure 3-1 Overall Process for Submitting a Master ROM (Overview). After reading this section, you will have a better grasp of how data is handled between the different steps.

The same overall workflow is also used to submit master ROMs for patches, downloadable content, and demo applications, although there are some small differences. For more information, see the corresponding pages for each procedure.

The process, through to the submission of a master ROM for applications, can be broken down broadly into the following three steps.

  • Submitting Usage Requests and Preparing the Development Environment
  • Creating a Master ROM
  • Submitting a Master ROM

This document describes how to proceed through these steps.
