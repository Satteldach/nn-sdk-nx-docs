2 Nintendo Developer Portal

The Nintendo Developer Portal is used to add application information configured to master ROMs, to receive application-specific IDs and versions from Nintendo, and to submit master ROMs and Lotcheck requests.

For more information about submitting master ROMs, see Nintendo Developer Portal > Help > My Products.
