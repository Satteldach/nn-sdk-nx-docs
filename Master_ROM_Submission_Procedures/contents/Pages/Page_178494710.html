6.6 Special ROM Submission Cases

This section describes special cases when submitting ROMs.

Precheck

Precheck refers to a Lotcheck process that tests ROMs for which development is incomplete for special reasons. You and Nintendo must agree in advance if you want to undergo precheck.

The submission procedure and required materials for precheck are nearly identical to those for submitting master ROMs. The following differences exist because development is incomplete for precheck ROMs.

  • Lotcheck requires strict compliance with the guidelines, or special approvals when those guidelines are not met. Some guidelines are exempt from compliance requirements for precheck, however.
  • For the Lotcheck process, Nintendo will inform you when the master ROM passes or fails. A pass is not possible for the precheck process, however.
