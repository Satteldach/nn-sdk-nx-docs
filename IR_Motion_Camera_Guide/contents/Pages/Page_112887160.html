2 Structure and Specifications

Hardware Configuration

The IR Motion Camera is built into the bottom of the Joy-Con (R).

A black covering over the IR Motion Camera cuts visible light and only allows infrared light to pass through.

Removing the black covering exposes the following mechanism.

The IR Motion Camera contains one IR Camera and four IR LEDs, as shown.


The IR LEDs emit infrared light, and the IR Camera records whatever the light illuminates.
The camera captures a monochrome image as shown in the following picture.

Applying different image processing to that monochrome image enables it to be used in a variety of situations. For more information, see 3 What the IR Motion Camera Can Do.
One characteristic of this mechanism is that it can operate normally in dim rooms and dark environments because it emits its own IR light.

IR Camera and IR LED Specifications

The main specifications of the IR Camera and IR LEDs are as follows.
The camera cannot capture color photos like a normal digital camera because it is not an RGB camera.

Option Specs Comments
IR Camera Resolution Maximum 320 × 240 (QVGA)

In general, a frame rate of 70 fps is used.

IR LED Field of View Angle at 50% Intensity and Number

Two 75° IR LEDs

Two 130° IR LEDs

Field of view angle at 50% intensity means that intensity of the IR LED observed at this angle is at half of its original intensity, and intensity does not reduce to 0 at this angle.
The range of light for a 75° IR LED is narrower, although it is a stronger light. The range for the light of a 130° IR LED is wider, but it is a weaker light.

IR Image Capture Range

In general, the camera can capture an IR image of an area within 30 cm directly in front of the camera, as in the following image.

The camera can record farther distances by using retroreflective material for the capture target.
However, this range changes with the camera's settings (including exposure time, digital gain, and switching the IR-LED on and off).
Exposure time, digital gain, and switching the IR-LED on and off are parameters that can be optionally configured. Use a range that extends to 30 cm directly in front of the camera when camera settings are at their maximum.
