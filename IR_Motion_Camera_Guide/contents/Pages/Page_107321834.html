1 Introduction

Introduction

This document describes the IR Motion Camera, which is a new feature appearing on a Nintendo gaming system for the first time. The IR Motion Camera uses infrared light (IR) and contains IR LEDs and an IR camera. Objects illuminated with the IR LEDs are captured with the IR camera to obtain an IR image. The hardware processes these IR images to recognize the movements and shapes of certain objects. Because it uses infrared, this feature also works in dark locations.

Intended Audience

This documentation is intended for planners and developers of Nintendo Switch™ (Switch) applications that use the IR Motion Camera.
