3.5 Following Nintendo Switch Terminology (Required)

Although it is not limited to controllers, follow the terminology included in Nintendo Switch Terminology.

Instructing Users to Attach or Detach Joy-Con Is Prohibited

For example, a user could prepare and use one Joy-Con while leaving another Joy-Con attached to the console for charging. In the case of Switch Lite, it is always in the state where the Joy-Con is attached. For this reason, when describing the play instructions, number of players, and Joy-Con grip technique within an application, do not provide instructions to attach or detach the Joy-Con. In this case, use any of the following expressions, which comply with the Nintendo Switch Terminology.

  • Prepare X (controller names).
  • Prepare (controller names) for X players.

The +Control Pad on Switch Lite and the Pro Controller

Use directional buttons as the collective term for the +Control Pad on Switch Lite and the Pro Controller and the directional buttons on the Joy-Con. Use the term directional buttons when the subject involves not just Switch Lite or the Pro Controller, but also involves the Joy-Con.
