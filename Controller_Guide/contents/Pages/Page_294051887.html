3.4 Displaying the Screen Showing and Describing How To Wear a Joy-Con Strap (Required)

From a safety viewpoint, the Joy-Con wrist strap must be used with applications in which the user might move the Joy-Con rapidly, broadly, and intensely. Display a screen that indicates to put on the Joy-Con wrist strap every time the application is started. For more information, see Nintendo Switch Application Safety Improvement Guide > Motion controls, and Nintendo Switch Guidelines > Safety > Applications that are played by moving the Joy-Con controllers > 0100: Explaining how to attach the Joy-Con strap.
