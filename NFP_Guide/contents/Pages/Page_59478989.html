6.8 Number of Times Writing to the Application-Specific Region

There is a limit to the number of times that the NFP tag or the device NAND memory may be written to. Please remember this limitation when you consider a design where the customer may write to the NFP tag repeatedly, such as a situation where writing to the application-specific region will drive customer satisfaction and happiness.

Writing to the NFP tag refers to altering the application-specific region using the Flush function. The product life may be lower than designed by Nintendo when the NFP is written to more than 100,000 times.

The NFP library also writes the backup data to the device NAND memory when writing to the application-specific region. An increase of write operations to the application-specific region also affects the life of the game console NAND memory.

The following examples show implementations that take into consideration the read/write cycle count.

  • Implementing a limit to the touches made per day
  • Consolidating Flush calls when writing to the amiibo figures as much as possible.
