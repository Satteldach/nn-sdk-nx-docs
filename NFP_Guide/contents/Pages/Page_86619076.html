2.2 What Is amiibo?

amiibo refers to a class of character products equipped with built-in NFP tags that can be read and written by Nintendo game systems and accessories. Examples of amiibo products include figures, cards, and stuffed toys.

Figure 2-2 amiibo Figures

In a sense, until now characters have been locked inside the game system. The purpose of NFP is to broaden the relationship with characters beyond and outside of the game system and to improve the game experience. To realize this objective, amiibo figures have the following features.

  • Physical, portable character figures
  • Characters users can call their own
Physical, Portable Character Products

Favorite characters in the form of figures. A non video-game product that can be by your side even when you are not playing video games. You can carry figures and show them to other people, creating opportunities for people to come in contact with game characters outside of the game machine, and engaging others in the fun of playing video games. Because the character figures also catch the eye of people unfamiliar with the video games, it creates opportunities to pique their interest in the games.

Because an amiibo figure can be played not only on the owner's game system, but also on other game systems, we envision friends carrying the figures around to play together at each other's homes.

Characters Users Can Call Their Own

Each amiibo product is registered with the owner's Mii and nickname. Application-specific data can also be written to the amiibo, enabling you to record what the amiibo has learned and how it has grown through the course of gameplay, and deepening the bond between the owner and the amiibo.

Regular figures cannot store experience, no matter how people play with them. But with amiibo figures, you can write applications that keep activity logs of the gameplay between owners and their favorite characters. The amiibo character itself grows the more the owner plays with the figure, becoming the owner's very own character.

Using the amiibo nickname and the owner's Mii registered on the amiibo itself, the two can call each other by name and clear challenges together inside the application, providing a player with the feeling that the amiibo is his or her partner.
