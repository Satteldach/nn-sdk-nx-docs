4.1.1 amiibo Usage Request

When developing an amiibo compatible application, please complete the amiibo Usage Application and Lotcheck Submission Sheet and submit it to Nintendo support. The following information must be provided on the form.

  • amiibo supported by the application
  • Intended use of the amiibo within the application

All amiibo characters that will be supported by the application must be accurately declared at the end of development. The list does not have to be final at the time of this initial request. When the amiibo functionality or design is altered during development of the application, please update and resubmit the amiibo usage request. Even when amiibo compatible content such as patches are updated after master submission, update and submit the request.
