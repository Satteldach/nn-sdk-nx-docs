6.11 Using the Touch Panel During amiibo Detection

On the New Nintendo 3DS and the New Nintendo 3DS XL, electromagnetic waves are generated during NFP tag detection. The touch panel cannot be used during amiibo detection due to the electronic interference created by these waves. The Nintendo 3DS, the Nintendo 3DS XL, and the Nintendo 2DS use a separate Nintendo 3DS NFC reader/writer accessory. As a result, there are no limitations on using the touch panel during amiibo detection.
