6.5 Display Position of Mii and Nickname

The nickname stored in the shared region is for that amiibo. When you show it in an application, be careful not to confuse it with the nickname for the owner's Mii.

Figure 6-2 Examples of Good and Bad Placement of Mii and Nickname presents examples of good and bad ways to display the owner's Mii and the nickname for the owner's amiibo, which in this case is "Peter." In the good example, the nickname and the owner's Mii are displayed in a way that makes it clear that the name of the amiibo is Peter. In the bad example, the nickname for the amiibo is shown directly above the Mii, suggesting that "Peter" is the nickname of the owner's Mii. Please do not display nicknames with this kind of placement.
