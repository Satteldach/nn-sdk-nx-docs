6.4 Using the Owner's Mii Characters and amiibo Nicknames

As described in 2.2.1 amiibo Figures and Their Owners, using an owner's Mii characters and amiibo nicknames creates a deeper relationship between users and their amiibo products and provides a better gaming experience.

We recommend that your amiibo compatible application includes features that take advantage of the Mii characters and amiibo nicknames registered on amiibo products to enhance the user experience. Providing more games that include such features encourages users to expand their amiibo collections and register their Mii characters and amiibo nicknames on their amiibo products, thereby helping to achieve the vision set out for the NFP platform in 2.2 What Is amiibo?.

Info

It does not matter whether the owner's Mii is shown as a Mii model, an icon, or a nickname.
