3 Using With Nintendo Switch Lite and Nintendo Switch - OLED Model

When Using a Toy-Con Accessory With Nintendo Switch Lite

You can use Toy-Con accessories with separate Joy-Con controllers.

However, Nintendo Switch Lite cannot be inserted in the following Toy-Con accessories, and the gaming experience would be different from what was originally intended.

  • Toy-Con Fishing Rod
  • Toy-Con House
  • Toy-Con Motorbike
  • Toy-Con Piano
  • Toy-Con Car

Also, Nintendo Switch Lite cannot be inserted in the following Toy-Con accessory, and that accessory cannot be used with Nintendo Switch Lite.

  • Toy-Con VR Goggles

When Using a Toy-Con Accessory With Nintendo Switch – OLED Model

The sizes of the console and screen differ between the Nintendo Switch [HAC-001][HAC-001(-01)] and Nintendo Switch – OLED Model [HEG-001].
