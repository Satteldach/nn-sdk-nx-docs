4 Ratings

Applications that support Toy-Con are preferably intended to be played by a wide age range of users.

For that reason, applications that support Toy-Con must obtain one of the ratings in the following table.

Rating Age Classification
CERO

ESRB

USK

PEGI

Russian

ACB

OFLC

GRAC

GSRR

IARC

Titles with excessive sexual content, violence, or horror elements may be rejected even if they fall within the rating range.

For applications that support the Toy-Con VR Goggles, the age rating for VR mode is "7 and older," so instruct users to restrict the use of VR mode by children ages 6 or younger.
