3.5 (Reference) Example of Sales of Bundled Downloadable Software and Downloadable Content

You can select various ways to sell even the same products, by making use of features such as buyer benefits and bundles.
This section uses the example of a combination of downloadable software and downloadable content, and introduces the possible product configurations.

Bundles of Downloadable Software and Downloadable Content

These bundles are sold alongside the downloadable software and downloadable content. Their strength is that the contents of each product included can be understood prior to purchase.
It is mainly used when selling downloadable content, that is also sold separately, in combination with the downloadable software.

Product Sold

  • Downloadable Software
    • Great Adventure ECG
  • Downloadable Content
    • Episode 1
    • Episode 2 (sales scheduled to start in the future)
  • Bundles
    • Great Adventure ECG Episode Set (Downloadable software + Episode 1 and Episode 2 Season Pass)

Granting Licenses to Get Downloadable Content for Downloadable Software

In this configuration, licenses for downloadable content are granted to purchasers of downloadable software as buyer benefits. It cannot be used for downloadable content provided as a special bonus and not otherwise sold. The downloadable content is displayed as a bonus after purchase.

Product Sold

  • Downloadable software
    • Great Adventure ECG (as a preorder bonus, rights to Episode 1 and Episode 2 downloadable content)

Sales of Downloadable Software and Downloadable Content as a Set

This sales format bundles downloadable content that is not sold individually with downloadable software for sale as a set. Unlike bundles, they appear as a single product to the user, as no additional content information is displayed during purchase.

Product Sold

  • Downloadable software
    • Great Adventure ECG - Episode 1 (Sold as set of downloadable software "Great Adventure ECG" and DLC "Episode 1")
  • Downloadable software
    • Great Adventure ECG - Episode 2 (Sold as set of downloadable software "Great Adventure ECG" and DLC "Episode 2")
