7.2.2 Monthly Format

Overview

Subscription services come in two formats: fixed term format and monthly format. For more information, see 7.1 Overview > Fixed Term Format and Monthly Format. This page describes how the monthly format differs from the fixed term format.

Mechanisms

The monthly format sells subscriptions in units of one calendar month. If a user purchases a one-month subscription service item, the valid period is between the day it was purchased and the same day of the next month. For example, if one-month subscription service items were purchased in April and in May, the validity period would be dated the same way, regardless of the number of days in the month, as shown below.

Date of Purchase Validity Period Notes
4/1 10:00 Until 5/1 09:59 There are 30 days in April.
5/1 10:00 Until 6/1 09:59

There are 31 days in May.

If the same day does not exist in the applicable month, the expiration date will be at the end of the month. The following are a few examples.

Date of Purchase Validity Period Notes
4/30 10:00 Until 5/30 09:59 There are 31 days in May.
5/31 10:00 Until 6/30 09:59 There are 30 days in June. Because there is no June 31, the subscription ends on June 30.

Automatic Renewal

Monthly format subscription service items may only be purchased with automatic renewal. Under conditions in which automatic renewal subscription service items cannot be sold, they are sold as subscription service items without automatic renewal. For more information about automatic renewal, see 7.2.1 Automatic Renewal.

Info

You cannot sell monthly format subscription service items in the following countries or regions that do not support automatic renewal items.

  • Korea
  • China

Updating the Automatic Renewal Agreement

As with fixed term format, the automatic renewal agreement is renewed if the remaining validity period is two days or less. If the update is successful, the expiration date is the same day of the next month. The following table shows examples of successful and unsuccessful updates (such as when the credit card transaction does not go through).

Date of Purchase Start Automatic Renewal Agreement Update Update Succeeded/Failed Validity Period
4/1 10:00 4/29 10:00 Succeeded Until 6/1 09:59
4/1 10:00 4/29 10:00 Failed Until 5/1 09:59
Info

Even if a grace period occurs and the automatic renewal agreement cannot be updated within two days, the expiration date is the originally scheduled date.

Date of Purchase and Updating the Automatic Renewal Agreement

The expiration date is one month from the date when the automatic renewal agreement was updated, regardless of the date of purchase. As indicated in the following examples, while the dates of purchase are different (5/30 and 5/31), the results are the same because the validity period is based on the day when the automatic renewal agreement was updated.

Date of Purchase Automatic Renewal Agreement Updated Validity Period Notes
5/30 10:00 6/30 10:00

Until 7/30 09:59

 May and July have 31 days, but June has 30 days.
5/31 10:00 6/30 10:00

Until 7/30 09:59

Additional Purchases

You cannot purchase additional subscription service items for the same course during the validity period.

Info

This specification may change in the future.

If the conditions for not being able to sell automatic renewal items apply, additional purchase is available. In this case, the maximum number of service days is the validity period maximum, as with the fixed term format.

Info

Additional purchases are no longer available when a user in Europe who is younger than 18 years old becomes 18 or older.

Resuming Update of the Automatic Renewal Agreement

If the user suspends update of the automatic renewal agreement in Nintendo eShop during the validity period, the automatic renewal contract can be resumed in Nintendo eShop.

Info

The automatic renewal contract cannot be resumed if the country/region of residence of the Nintendo Account was changed after suspending update of the automatic renewal agreement.

Feature for Getting Information Within Applications

As with fixed term format, the same feature for getting information within applications is available. Note that the body of the response of the API for getting the list of subscription service items differs between fixed term format and monthly format. For more information, see 8.5 Subscription Service Items.

Note

When getting subscription service information using the EcCatalog sample for NX Add-On versions earlier than 10.4.0, an error is returned for monthly format items because they are not supported. Use the EcCatalog sample in NX Add-On 10.4.0 and later.

Displaying Subscription Service Items That Are Not Available for Additional Purchases

The list of items that can be obtained using the API for getting a list of subscription service items also includes items unavailable for purchase because there is an existing validity period. To hide these items, you must have an implementation that does not display items if there is an existing validity period.

Restrictions

  • You cannot sell items with durations other than 1 month (for example, 3 months).
  • Download codes cannot be used.
  • Automatic renewal items for free trials may not be sold.

How to Use

The process for OMAS requests is the same as the process for the fixed term format. For more information about OMAS requests, see 4.1 Usage Request on OMAS.

When registering a new course in NCMS, set Grant Time Minimum Setting to Months to indicate a monthly format item, and set it to Minutes to indicate a fixed term item. This setting cannot be changed. In addition, you cannot register a subscription service item with a format different from the configured format. In other words, you cannot mix monthly format items and fixed term items in the same course.
