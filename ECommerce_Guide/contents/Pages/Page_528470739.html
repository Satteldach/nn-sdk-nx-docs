2.2 Jump to Nintendo eShop

Jump to eShop refers to the feature for displaying product details or a list of products in Nintendo eShop from within the application, in order to direct users to purchase products. To use the Jump to eShop feature, call the SDK SPI (nn::ec) within the application to start the EC applet.

Figure 2-1 Starting the EC Applet From Within the Application

You can use this feature to jump to pages on Nintendo eShop for various purposes, including displaying the details about downloadable software, lists of downloadable content, and lists of specific downloadable content specified by the API.

For more information about the correspondence between the API functions for jumping to eShop, the landing pages, and the products accessible after the jump, see 9.3 Supplemental Information and Considerations on Jump to eShop. Important information about implementing jumps to eShop is also presented.
