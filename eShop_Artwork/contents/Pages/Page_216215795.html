4.2 Logos (for the Nintendo 3DS Family)

This section describes the Nintendo eShop logos for the Nintendo 3DS family.

Logo Design

Filename: Nintendo eShop_logo_XXX_YYYMMDD_3DS_WIiU.ai

Color

Use Nintendo Grey for "Nintendo" and Nintendo Network Orange for "eShop" for the Nintendo eShop logo.

Rules for Using Artwork

Minimum Margin

When displaying the Nintendo eShop logo, set a fixed minimum margin around the icon to prevent it from merging with other notation. As a general rule, we do not recommend placing words or figures within the area.

Ensuring Visibility

We recommend using the shadows or glow effects (as shown in the following image) when placing the logo on an illustrated, colored, or patterned background, where visibility of the logo cannot be ensured.
