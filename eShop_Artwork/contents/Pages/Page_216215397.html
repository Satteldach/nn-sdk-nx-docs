3.1 Icons (for Switch)

This section describes the icons for Nintendo eShop for the Switch.

eShop Icons

Filename: Nintendo eShop_ icon_XXX_YYMMDD_Switch .ai

Acceptable Uses

  • Representing Nintendo eShop
  • Placement of items such as link buttons
  • Indicating that products are sold on Nintendo eShop

Rules for Using Artwork

  • Use the logo without making changes.
  • Use the basic colors on this page.
  • Maintain the aspect ratio when reducing or enlarging the image size.

Exceptions to the Rules

Colors and lines can be arranged as exceptions only when you must match the game appearance or product image. The following are examples of such alterations.

  • Matching by changing colors or adding patterns
  • Matching by adding shadows, glow effects, textures, or borders.
  • Matching by simplifying the artwork by using just its outline, or removing the outline entirely
  • Matching by changing to a more digital or hand-drawn feel
  • Make sure not to make changes that would prevent people from associating the icon with a shopping bag.
