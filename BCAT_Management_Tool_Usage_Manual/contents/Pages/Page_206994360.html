3.2 Related Products

Related Products

You can post links to Nintendo eShop products in news items. This feature is called Related Products.

A maximum of 10 related products can be posted in a single news item.

Related Products That Can Be Posted

The following three criteria must be met when posting a product as a related product.

1. Approval

Approval from Nintendo in NCMS is required before posting a product as a related product.

Products that have not been approved do not appear as related product candidates in the BCAT Management Tool.

2. Delivery Country

When posting products as related products, all related products must be delivered in all countries that receive the news item.

For example, we assume that the News items are being delivered to CA (Canada) and to MX (Mexico).

Additionally, we assume that Product-1 and Product-2 will be sold as shown in the following table.

Delivery Country Product-1 Product-2
CA Sell. Sell.
MX Sell. Do not sell.

In this case, only Product-1 can be posted as a related product.

Because Product-2 is not sold in MX, it cannot be posted as a related product.

Product-2 appears as a candidate in the BCAT Management Tool and can be selected, but the news item cannot be saved if it is selected.

Complete either of the following steps to post Product-2.

  • Change the BCAT Management Tool settings, and do not deliver to MX.
  • Change the settings in NCMS, and sell Product-2 to MX.

3. Release Start Date and Time

When posting products as related products, the delivery of the news item must start after the product is released on Nintendo eShop.

The example in the following table is based on two products, sold in two countries, posted as related products.

Product Delivery Country Release Start Date and Time Set in NCMS
Product-1 CA

Beginning on 2017-06-01
US Beginning on 2017-07-01
Product-2 CA Beginning on 2017-08-01
US Beginning on 2017-09-01

In this case, we recommend starting news delivery on or after 2017-09-01, the date when all related products have been released in all delivery countries.

Even if a product has not yet been released on Nintendo eShop, it is displayed as a related product candidate in the BCAT Management Tool. However, with the following exceptions, you cannot save a News items with these kinds of products selected.

Note

This section describes exceptions.

Due to system constraints in the BCAT Management Tool, in the case described previously, you can start news delivery on or after 2017-08-01, when all related products have been released in either country.

For example, in this case, if you select Product-2 from News prior to 2017-09-01 and transition to Nintendo eShop using a US Nintendo Account, an error occurs because the product has not been released yet. Keep that in mind.

When posting related products, we recommend using Nintendo Accounts for all countries to which news will be delivered, and confirming that you can transition to Nintendo eShop from the news items delivered during testing.

Differences From Link

3.1 Link is a feature that allows for more freedom, compared to Related Products.

Although a maximum of 10 related products can be posted in a single news item, only one link can be posted in each news item.
