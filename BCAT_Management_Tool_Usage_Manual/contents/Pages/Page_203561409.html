2.4 Nintendo Review - Tencent Review

Nintendo Review - Tencent Review

For the delivery of News items and data, you need not only a 2.3 Pre-Delivery Review, but also a process of confirmation known as either a Nintendo review or a Tencent review.

These reviews are meant to confirm the following.

  • Each News item was created in accordance with the Nintendo Switch News Delivery Guide.
  • (Only for applications for China) There are no problems with the data that will be delivered through BCAT data delivery.
Info
  • Applications for China are applications for which the sales region has been set to China on the Nintendo Developer Portal.
  • If an application is not an application for China, no review is conducted on that data.

News Reviews

News Subject to Review

Whether a News item is subject to a Nintendo Review or a Tencent Review is determined based on the Delivery Region where the News item will be delivered.

News Delivery Region Nintendo Review Tencent Review
NOE (Nintendo of Europe) regions (Europe and Australia) Review conducted Review not conducted
NOA (Nintendo of America) regions (North and South America) Review not conducted Review not conducted
NCL (Nintendo Co., Ltd.) region (Japan) Review not conducted Review not conducted
NOK (Nintendo of Korea) region (Korea) Review not conducted Review not conducted
NHL (Nintendo Hong Kong) region (Hong Kong) Review conducted Review not conducted
Tencent region (China) Review not conducted Review conducted

For example, if a News item will be delivered to the United States (US) and Mexico (MX), neither a Nintendo Review nor a Tencent Review is conducted.

Status Transition

The Nintendo reviewer approves or rejects News with a Nintendo review requested status. Similarly, the Tencent reviewer approves or rejects News with a Tencent review requested status.

The diagram shows the relationship between the status and the operations of the different users.

The Editor in the diagram represents the user who created the News item. The Reviewer represents the user who performs the pre-delivery review. Additionally, Nintendo News Reviewer and Tencent News Reviewer on the diagram indicate the people in charge of reviewing at Nintendo or Tencent.

Data Reviews

In the exclusive case of applications for China, Tencent reviews the data that will be delivered via BCAT data delivery.
