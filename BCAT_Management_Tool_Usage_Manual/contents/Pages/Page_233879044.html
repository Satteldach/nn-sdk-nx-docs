2.5 Email Notifications

Email Notifications

When important operations are performed on the BCAT Management Tool, notifications are sent to the email addresses associated with the NDIDs of the concerned users.

Email notifications are sent only in the dd1 and lp1 server environments.

Filtering

You can perform the following operations in the Mail Settings menu.

  • Turn off email notifications for News delivery to specific countries. Or turn them on.
    • For example, use this feature when your channel has different members handling delivery for America and delivery for Japan, and each member does not need to receive email notifications for the other countries.
  • Turn off all other notifications. Or turn them on.
    • This applies, for example, to notifications about role change requests or data delivery.

Sender Address

The sender address of email notifications varies depending on the BCAT Management Tool server environment.

Automatic Checking for Inappropriate Wording (News for China Only)

In a process that is limited to News items for China, all saved News items are automatically checked periodically for inappropriate wording (with the Dirty Word filter). If the News is found to contain inappropriate wording, the status for the News is rolled back to Editing and notification is sent via email.

This check is also performed when the News is being saved, but email notification is not sent if inappropriate wording is found at that time. For more information about this check for inappropriate wording, see Nintendo Switch News Delivery Guide > Notes About Creating News.
