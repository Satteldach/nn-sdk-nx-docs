Revision History

Version 13.0 (2022-03-22)

Changes

  • 4 Notes About Creating News
    • Reorganized the text describing the operation rules for all regions in Prohibited Content.
    • In Prohibited Content, in the operation rules that are specific to Japan, added a rule that prohibits announcements of media products with older age ratings than the rating for the software itself.
    • In Prohibited Content, in the operation rules that are specific to Japan, Korea, Hong Kong, and China, added a rule that prohibits announcements of initiatives that come with conditions when steering readers to external media.
    • In Prohibited Content, in the operation rules that are specific to Japan, Korea, Hong Kong, and China, added a rule that prohibits announcements of owner discounts.
    • In Prohibited Content, in the operation rules that are specific to the Americas, Europe, and Australia, added a rule that prohibits announcements of some kinds of owner discounts.
    • In Prohibited Content, in the operation rule that prohibits mentioning sales initiatives other than those on Nintendo eShop, for regions other than Europe and Australia, changed "other than Nintendo eShop" to "Other companies and the media of other companies".
    • In Prohibited Content, in the operation rules that are specific to Japan, Hong Kong, and China, added an example of exceptional cases where sales announcements are allowed to the rule that prohibits content that would encourage new purchases of the software, including information about discounted pricing, after the software is released.
    • In Prohibited Content, in the section about Nintendo's response to News items that contain prohibited content and expressions, added that Nintendo might temporarily stop delivery.
    • In Important Information, added an operation rule specific to Japan that notes that only the CERO rating should be set if the application gets both the CERO rating and the IARC rating.
    • In Important Information, added an operation rule that is specific to the Americas that notes that both the ESRB rating and the SMEC rating should be set if Mexico is included among the recipient countries for the News.
  • 5.1 Applying for a Channel
    • Moved the Applying for a Channel section to the top half of the page.
    • Moved the text explaining that the channel icon will be overwritten by the icon in a later-approved channel request from Applying for a Channel > Notes to the description for Channel Icon in the table in Materials Required to Apply for a Channel.
    • Noted that the channel name displayed in the BCAT Management Tool reflects the name of the setting in the region where the News channel request was approved last.
