1 Introduction

Purpose and Content

This document describes the specifications, the delivery method, and the rules for News, one of the system features of the Nintendo Switch™ console (Switch).

Intended Audience

This document is intended for the following audiences.

  • People considering delivering News
  • People responsible for News delivery operations

How to Read This Documentation

  1. For a general understanding of News, see 2 News and 3 UI Reference.
  2. To decide whether you need to create News and deliver it, see 4 Notes About Creating News.
  3. For more information about creating and delivering News, see the following.
    • 5 Workflow for News Delivery
    • 6 BCAT Management Tool: Setting Items and Setting Rules

This document describes the rules that vary by region. Where applicable, the descriptions are marked as Japan-Specific, Americas-Specific, Europe-Specific, Australia-Specific, Korea-Specific, Hong Kong-Specific, or China-Specific. Follow the rules for the regions where News will be delivered.

Request for Compliance With This Documentation

If News items that do not comply with the contents of this documentation are delivered, Nintendo may request revisions to those items. If this occurs, make prompt revisions.

If you do not make prompt revisions and there are no extenuating circumstances, the following ban measures may be applied.

  • Immediately stop delivery of a News item.
  • Ban use of the News feature for individual channels (temporary or permanent ban).
  • Ban use of the News feature for individual publisher (temporary or permanent ban).

Depending on the content of the News item, these measures may be implemented immediately without going through the revision request process. Be careful when you create and deliver News items because many users will see them. If you are unclear about these rules, or if there are cases where content or settings are difficult to judge, contact Nintendo support ahead of time.

Glossary

  • NCMS
    NCMS stands for Nintendo Content Management System, the name of the management tool used to submit information and materials required for delivery to Nintendo eShop.
  • BCAT
    BCAT stands for Background Contents Asymmetric Sync Delivery and Transmission and is a system for implementing data delivery.
  • BCAT Management Tool
    The web tool for creating and managing News and data for delivery to the Switch system.
