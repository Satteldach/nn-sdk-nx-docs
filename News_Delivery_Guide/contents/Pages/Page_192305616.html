2 News

News is a feature for delivering information about the Switch directly to the user's Switch system via the internet. News entries comprise images (or videos) and text and are displayed to users.

News is not delivered to devices that are not connected to the internet. (Some consoles include News items when purchased.)

Download Criteria

News is delivered to each console rather than to each user account on the console or each Nintendo Account associated with the user accounts. News that has been downloaded can be viewed by any user account on the system.

All of the following criteria must be satisfied to download News items.

  • The user account is associated with a Nintendo Account. (If there are multiple user accounts on the Nintendo Switch, downloads work if any one of the user accounts is associated with a Nintendo Account.)
  • The device is connected to the internet.
  • The News channel is being followed.

News will be downloaded if the console meets the preceding criteria and the target country for News delivery matches the country setting of any Nintendo Account linked to that console.

Delivered News items can be viewed even when not connected to the internet. (Excludes viewing videos.)

Info

Only the Nintendo Switch Information Center, a channel described later, delivers News to all Switch consoles connected to the internet, regardless of whether they are linked to a Nintendo Account.

On the Channel Details screen, you can view all currently available News items on that channel.

To view these News items, you must be linked to a Nintendo Account and have an internet connection, but you do not need to follow the channel.

For more information about Channel Details, see 3 UI Reference.

Display Language

The display language for downloaded News items is the language that was selected by the user in the system settings. It is not the language selected for the Nintendo Account.

You can configure an alternate language for each News item. If an alternate language has been set, the console can download News in that language when there is no available News in the language set in the system settings. We recommend using the default alternate language. Set the alternate language in the BCAT Management Tool.

Info

If the language is changed in the system settings after downloading News, News that was previously downloaded remains in the language from when it was downloaded.

Channels

The unit of delivery for News is called a channel. The following types of channels are available.

Nintendo News - The channel for game information

Delivers a variety of different information about applications, peripherals, and events for the Nintendo Switch. Users can opt out of receiving this information.

Nintendo eShop Guide - The channel for the latest information from Nintendo eShop

Delivers a variety of News about Nintendo eShop, including information about new software and downloadable content, sales, and demo software. Users can also opt out of receiving this information.

Nintendo Switch Information Center - The channel for system information

Delivers a variety of News about the Switch system, including notices of system updates, support issues, tips, and usage alerts. This channel is delivered to all Switch consoles connected to the internet.

Channels for Specific Software

Publishers that release applications for Switch can set up one channel for each software title, at their discretion.

This capability allows publishers to directly send software News to users, which can include notices for in-game events and the availability of downloadable content. After the channel has been established, News can be delivered to the systems of users who have played that software and users who voluntarily follow the channel. Users can also unfollow channels on a channel-by-channel basis.

News items marked as [Europe-Specific], [Australia-Specific], [Hong Kong-Specific], and [China-Specific] are subject to a separate review by Nintendo or Tencent. For more information, see 5 Workflow for News Delivery.

How Users Add Channels

Nintendo News and Nintendo eShop Guide

The Nintendo News channel is followed by default when the preceding criteria in Download Criteria are met.

Channels can be followed and unfollowed in News Channel Settings in the system settings.

Channels for Specific Software

To follow the channel for a specific software title, one of the following conditions must be satisfied in addition to all of the conditions described in Download Criteria on this page.

  • The software was started after the date and time when the channel was established.
  • The user has voluntarily chosen to follow the channel.

Users can configure the following or unfollowing of channels using the follow/unfollow button in either the Channel List or the Channel Details.
