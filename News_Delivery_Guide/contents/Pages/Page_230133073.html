4 Notes About Creating News

This section describes considerations for News delivery, provides recommendations and prohibitions about the content of News items, and also includes other important notes.

Recommended Content

Channels for specific software deliver News to the Switch systems where that software has been run, and systems that follow that channel.

Use the following descriptions of content as a guide to create and deliver News items that are related to the relevant software.

News can be delivered both before and after the software is released. If the News is delivered after software release, when you determine the content of News items, consider the fact that News may be delivered to users who are playing the software.

Strongly Recommended Content

We strongly recommend delivering News for the following events.

  • Beginning delivery of downloadable content (including information about DLC sold as a set) and selling DLC
  • Releasing patches
Recommended Content

We recommend delivering News in the following cases.

  • Content for the applicable software, such as announcing in-game events, game strategy, trivia to enhance enjoyment of the game, videos introducing the game
  • Introductions to merchandise for the software itself
  • Thanks and greetings to purchasers
  • Introduction to download codes for items that would be advantageous in the game.

Prohibited Content

The following content is prohibited because it goes against the purpose of channels for specific software.

  • Announcements about sequels to the software are prohibited, as are announcements about series products, related products, bundles that do not include the software, different software for sale by the same publisher, applications for smart devices, IP information, and software from other publishers.
  • Although announcements about IP are generally prohibited, announcements about media products based on the software such as movies, animations, and manga are allowed to be presented in the channel, but only once.
  • Although mentioning the software of other publishers is generally prohibited, it is allowed in cases where there are collaborations between products and the other software needs to be mentioned to introduce this software.
  • [Japan-Specific] As noted, announcements of media products based on the software are allowed, but introducing media products with older age ratings than the rating of the software itself is prohibited.
  • Although announcements of sequels, series products, and related products are generally prohibited, they are allowed if they enhance the game experience of the software, such as when a sequel-related event is conducted inside the software.
  • Although announcements of applications for smart devices are generally prohibited, mentioning smart device applications and products like toys is allowed, as long as there is data linked to the software and there is clear benefit in the software for the user.
  • Announcements about real events and surveys specific to the software are allowed. Bear in mind that users not living near the event venues might also be viewing the announcements about real events.
  • [Japan, Americas, Korea, Hong Kong, and China-Specific] Mentions of sales initiatives by retailers, other companies, and the media of other companies (such as announcements of limited sales by retailers and pre-order purchase benefits) are prohibited.
  • [Europe, Australia-Specific] Mentions of sales initiatives by retailers, other companies, and the media of other companies (such as announcements of limited sales by retailers and pre-order purchase benefits) are prohibited. In addition, announcements of sales initiatives in Nintendo media other than Nintendo eShop is prohibited.
  • [Japan, Korea, Hong Kong, China-Specific] In steering readers to external media other than News, announcements of initiatives that come with conditions, such as the need to create an account for the external media, are prohibited. Users who have viewed the News in the channel for the software are allowed to announce condition-free initiatives, as well as questionnaires and the like.
  • [Japan, Hong Kong, China-Specific] Users who purchased the software are assumed to be receiving News for that software. For this reason, content that would encourage new purchases of that software, including information about discounted pricing for the software itself after that software is released, is prohibited. However, announcements can be made of sales in situations where the software cannot be purchased at a fixed price. For example, it is all right to announce a sale if there are no pre-orders and the sale is implemented at the same time that the software is released, or if the sale will run from the start of pre-orders until after the release of the software.
  • [Japan, Korea, Hong Kong, China-Specific] Announcements of owner discounts is prohibited.
  • [Americas, Europe, Australia-Specific] Announcements of owner discounts that offer other software at lower price to owners of this software is prohibited. However, announcements of owner discounts that offer this software at lower price to owners of other software is allowed.
  • [Japan-Specific] News delivery to Japan in languages other than Japanese is prohibited.
  • [Korea-Specific] News delivery to Korea in languages other than Korean is prohibited.
  • [Hong Kong-Specific] News delivery to Hong Kong in languages other than Traditional Chinese is prohibited.
  • [China-Specific] News delivery to China in languages other than Simplified Chinese is prohibited.
  • [Europe-Specific] [Australia-Specific] Using wording that directly prompts minors to make purchases is prohibited.

News items are delivered to the Switch system and not to a specific user account. Considering that News may be viewed by users across a broad age range, the following content and expressions are prohibited in News items. If News items contain these prohibited kinds of content and expressions, Nintendo may request that you revise the News items and may even temporarily stop delivery. Consult with Nintendo if you are unsure whether the item contains prohibited content or language. For contact information, see the 1 Introduction.

Important Information

Frequency and Period of Delivery

  • Adjust the schedule so that no more than 10 News items are in circulation simultaneously for a single game channel. The purpose is to prevent a large number of News items from being delivered simultaneously to Switch systems that have connected to the internet or linked a Nintendo Account for the first time.
  • Although there are no particular restrictions on the period set for delivery, follow the instructions described later in Setting the Delivery Period and Display Period for News.

[Europe-Specific] [Australia-Specific] [Hong Kong-Specific] For News for these regions, deliver no more than ten News items over any seven-day period.

Setting the Delivery Period and Display Period for News

  • For News items that use transient phrases (like "Animation playing now!"), always configure the delivery end date and display period to fall within the target period.
  • Configure the delivery end date and time such that it falls on or before the end of any limited-time sales you want to introduce. This prevents the delivery of new sale information after the sale has ended. You can also set a display deadline if you do not want the News item to be visible to the user after the sale has ended.
  • If the News items notify users of limited-time benefits, in-game events and the like, stop delivery of the News after the corresponding time period has ended unless it is absolutely necessary not to do so.
    To avoid the situation where a large number of News items for a channel keep being sent, set a display deadline for News items that would be of no advantage to users after they have arrived (or been viewed) so that those News items are no longer shown on the device.

Setting the Delivery Period and Display Period for News Containing Video Clips

  • If the News item contains a video (as the main video or related video), make sure the delivery period and display period for the News item falls within the release period set for the video in NCMS.
  • Videos all reference NCMS. If the release period set in NCMS has passed (or has not yet come), the video cannot be played from the News item and an error is displayed. Pay attention to the video availability period settings in NCMS when setting a video in the News item.

Notes on Nintendo Switch Lite

  • News items are sent to both Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch Lite devices. Note: News items cannot be specifically sent to only one device or the other.
  • Add an appropriate notice to the footer of the News items to avoid purchase confusion if gameplay would be restricted on either device or would require the additional purchase of an accessory.
  • Text used in a list image or in the main image might be harder to read on the Nintendo Switch Lite because its screen size is different from that of the Nintendo Switch. We strongly recommend test delivery to both devices before actually delivering the News item.

[China-Specific] The Nintendo Switch Lite is not currently being sold in China.

Content Containing Copyrighted Materials (Such as Music)

When including copyrighted materials in News to be delivered, the publisher of the News is responsible for clearing the usage rights ahead of time. Be careful not to violate any applicable laws.

[Japan-Specific] If information for a copyrighted musical composition is registered on NCMS and the domestic interactive distribution rights for that composition are managed by JASRAC (the Japanese Society for Rights of Authors, Composers and Publishers) or NexTone, Nintendo will confirm whether it can be used in the News item and check the details.

Posting QR Codes Within News Items

  • The inclusion of a QR Code is only permitted within the main image. Note: Their inclusion is prohibited in list images.
  • Make sure the target destination for any QR Code included in a main image is correct and functioning before actually delivering the News item. Test for delivery to both devices before actually delivering the News item to make sure that the QR Code is accurately read from both screens.
  • The QR Code registered trademark does not need to appear in individual News items.

[Americas-, Europe-, Australia-, and Hong Kong-Specific] Considering Recipients for Expressions of Dates and Seasons

Dates are formatted differently in different countries.

  • The US format is 3/4/2017.
  • The UK format is 4/3/2017.

We recommend using the following date formats when delivering the same News to the Americas, Europe, and Australia. Use a format that considers the recipient.

  • US format: March 4, 2017.
  • UK format: 4 March 2017.

Also consider the recipient when using seasonal expressions. Even though the date would be the same, the seasons are reversed in the northern and southern hemispheres.

[Hong Kong-Specific]: For Hong Kong-specific News, use the UK format for dates.

[Americas-, Europe-, and Australia-Specific] Currency Representations That Consider the Recipient

The currency differs according to the target country when delivering News at a language level. We recommend using currency symbols and languages appropriate to the target country when you list a price.

[Americas-Specific] Including Canada as a Delivery Target

If one of the recipient countries for the News is Canada, make sure that you also set text in French using the BCAT Management Tool.

[Americas-Specific] Including Mexico as a Delivery Target

If Mexico is included among the recipient countries for the News, set both the ESRB rating and the SMEC rating.

[Japan-Specific] Getting Multiple Ratings

If the application gets both the CERO rating and the IARC rating, set only the CERO rating. Be careful not to set both the CERO and IARC rating.

Using Images That Do Not Display a Ratings Symbol

Rating organizations differ by country and region. The symbol for the rating organizing appears in places such as the application package image.

When using images for News, we recommend using images that do not include a rating organization symbol.

[China Specific] Detection of Inappropriate Wording

In a process that is specific to News for China, the wording in the News is checked at the following times.

  • When News items are added and updated
  • During periodic automatic checks

Until this check has been completed, neither the process of confirming before actual delivery nor actual delivery can be performed. If the News contains inappropriate wording, neither the process of confirming before actual delivery nor actual delivery can be performed until that wording has been corrected.

The periodic auto-checking is conducted on all News for China that has been saved. If the News is found to contain inappropriate wording, the status for the News is rolled back to Editing and the person in charge is notified by email.

Wording that is deemed inappropriate might be added or changed at any time.
