5.5 Revisions After Delivery

This section describes the process of revising the News after the start of delivery.

Revising News Items

If you want to revise a News item after it has been released, overwrite the News content and then deliver the News item again.

Note

If the News is specific to Europe, Australia, or Hong Kong, the Nintendo review process also applies to this overwrite of the News item.

If the News is specific to China, the Tencent review process also applies to this overwrite of the News item.

Contact the corresponding customer support regional contact if you require an urgent review by Nintendo or Tencent.

Starting a Fix

After selecting News in the BCAT Management Tool, perform one of the following, depending on when delivery ends.

The End Date and Time Has Not Yet Passed

Begin by clicking the Stop Delivery button.

Click the Edit button next.


The End Date and Time Has Already Passed

Click the Modify Content button.

Correcting Misprints

If you find errors after a News item has been delivered, you can overwrite the content and deliver the corrected version of the News item.

A News item can be overwritten on the user's Switch system regardless of whether the News item has been read. The updated version is not treated as a new arrival, and the user is not notified that it is an update of an existing News item.

For this reason, if you are making a revision to an important matter, we recommend mentioning the modification. You can, for example, include the following at the beginning of the News item.

The News item delivered on X/X at XX:XX contained an error. The correct content is as follows.

Deleting News in the Hands of Users

If you want to not only stop the delivery of a News item but also remove that item, specify a display term.

If you set a date in the past for this display term and deliver News again to overwrite the News item, it will no longer be shown on the user's Switch system.

We recommend extending the delivery end date and time for that News item so the user has more opportunity to download the updated News item.

Note
  • It may be several hours or longer before the user downloads the updated News item.
  • There is no assurance that all users can download the updated News item.
    • For example, if a Switch system that has received an erroneous News item remains offline past the date and time set for delivery to end, that News item will not be revised (or not be hidden) on that system.
    • Consider overwriting News items as a way to minimize the effect.
  • After the delivered news display term set for a News item has passed, that News item will be deleted from the Switch and will not be redelivered. If you want to extend the display term for a News item, make sure that you revise the setting before the term has passed.
