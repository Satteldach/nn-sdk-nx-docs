5.3 Confirming Before Actual Delivery

This section describes the confirmation process prior to actual News delivery.

For more information about using the BCAT Management Tool, see the BCAT Management Tool Operations Manual.

Consistency With Delivery Status

If the News item contains an expression like "available now," or "distribution to start," or "on sale now," make sure that you confirm the following conditions.

  • The relevant product (such as a game or downloadable content) or patch is being delivered in the production environment.
  • The discount for the relevant product is applied in Nintendo eShop.
Note

If the News item contains any of the preceding kinds of expressions, making a reservation to deliver the News item (pre-approved delivery) is prohibited until the delivery status of the product or patch on the Switch system is confirmed.

The purpose of this prohibition is to prevent inconsistencies between the News item and the delivery status of the subject of the News.

Previewing in the BCAT Management Tool

Use the BCAT Management Tool to preview the News item after you have created it using the tool.

Use the preview to check on the following points.

  • The content (both the body text and images) is laid out as intended.
  • There are no typos or omissions in the body text in any of the languages.

Test Delivery on the Switch System (Test Delivery on a Retail Unit)

After you have saved the News item with the BCAT Management Tool, conduct a test delivery to a Switch system.

Use test delivery to check the following points.

  • Line breaks are placed as intended.
    • The preview feature of the BCAT Management Tool does not accurately reproduce the positions of line breaks as displayed on the Switch.
  • Still images are displayed normally.
  • Videos are played normally.
  • The names of related videos are displayed normally.
  • Clicking the link button below the body text displays the associated page correctly.
  • Clicking the link to a related product to the right of the text displays the associated page correctly.
  • Text where tags are used is displayed as intended.
    • Tags can only be used in the body. If tags are used in other text, it is displayed as is without the tags being processed.
    • Only up to two lines of text surrounded in <header></header> are displayed, so watch for text being cut off.
Note

If you are delivering the News items to multiple countries, carefully check the behavior of the link button and the links to related products.

The sales status for a particular item of content may be different in different countries, so the linked page may not be displayed properly (for example, due to an error indicating that the product has not yet been released).

Sharing Review Tasks

If multiple people are responsible for a channel, we recommend that multiple people review the project where necessary (confirm with test delivery).

For example, if the roles of editing and approving are held by different people, we recommend that both parties review the project.

Reviews by Nintendo and Tencent

Use the BCAT Management Tool to request a review from Nintendo or Tencent after the process of confirming before actual delivery has been conducted by the person in charge of the channel.

If you have questions about the review process, use the contacts listed in the 1 Introduction.


Note the following information about the review process.

  • You will be notified of the results of the review by email.
  • Nintendo or Tencent might request revisions. If this occurs, make prompt revisions.
  • After the Nintendo or Tencent review has been approved, News delivery begins on the date set for the start of delivery.
  • If the Nintendo or Tencent review is not approved before the date set for the start of delivery, News delivery begins after approval is obtained.

[Europe, Australia, and Hong Kong-Specific] Requesting a Nintendo Review

In the case of content for Europe, Australia, and Hong Kong, you must request a review by Nintendo.

Reviews by Nintendo are estimated to take approximately one to five days. Submit your request well in advance.

[China-Specific] Requesting a Tencent Review

In the case of content for China, you must request a review by Tencent.

Reviews by Tencent are estimated to take approximately one to five days. Submit your request well in advance.
