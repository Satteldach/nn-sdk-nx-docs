5.4 Actual Delivery

This section describes the actual delivery of the News item.

Procedure for Actual Delivery

Japan-, Americas-, and Korea-Specific News

  1. Have a responsible party for the channel carry out the pre-delivery review.
  2. After the pre-delivery review has been approved, delivery starts at the delivery start date and time.

Europe-, Australia-, and Hong Kong-Specific News

  1. Have a responsible party for the channel carry out the pre-delivery review.
  2. After the pre-delivery is approved, the Nintendo review takes place.
  3. After the Nintendo review has been approved, delivery starts at the delivery start date and time.

China-Specific News

  1. Have a responsible party for the channel carry out the pre-delivery review.
  2. After the pre-delivery review is approved, the Tencent review takes place.
  3. After the Tencent review has been approved, delivery starts at the delivery start date and time.

Procedure After Actual Delivery

After the date and time set for the start of delivery has passed, make sure that you confirm that actual delivery can be received, using a Switch system different from the one that was used for the pre-delivery check.

It might take time to receive the actual delivery of the News item.

If three hours have passed after the delivery start date and time and you cannot receive the actual delivered News item, check the following.

  • Switch system settings
  • News settings in BCAT Management Tool
