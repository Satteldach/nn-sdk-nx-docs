6 Supporting Each Play Mode

This section primarily describes how to support "Policy 1" in the basic policies, and what to do if supporting Policy 1 is not possible.

For information about the differences in performance among the play modes (TV mode, tabletop mode, and handheld mode) that all Switch applications that support basic policies must support, see 6.1 Absorbing the Performance Differential.

For additional specifications about how to use the touch panel or two Joy-Con controllers separately, see 6.2 Using Features That Vary by Play Mode.

For information about how to determine the supported play modes and how to describe the play modes to the users, see 6.3 Information for Users of Supported Play Modes and Definitions of Those Modes.
