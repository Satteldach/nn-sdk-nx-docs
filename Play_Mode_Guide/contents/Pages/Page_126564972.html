4 Changing Play Modes

As you connect and disconnect the Joy-Con and Switch dock to and from the Switch, you can change between different play modes without needing to carry around special peripherals.

The following examples show scenarios that include a change in play modes.

Figure 4-1 Example of Changing From Tabletop Mode to TV Mode

This example depicts a case where two brothers are playing on the Switch in tabletop mode when a family member, the mother in this illustration, leaves the home and makes the TV available. At that point, the brothers can place the Switch console in the Switch dock and continue playing on the larger screen.

The Wii U allowed the user to take just the GamePad out of the living room, and when the television became available, the players could play the rest of the game using the TV.

Switch expands on this characteristic. Because you take the entire Switch system with you, there are no limitations based on factors such as the range of the Wi-Fi signal. You can keep having fun even during multiplayer games.

Because Switch does not use the TV screen and the console screen at the same time (if there is only one device), we expect that many titles will make use of this characteristic.

Figure 4-2 Changing From TV Mode to Handheld Mode

This example shows someone playing with the Switch at home, using the television. He notices that it is time to leave. He removes the Switch system from the Switch dock, connects the Joy-Con, and takes the whole thing with him, enabling him to play even while riding the train.

The Switch enables you to have fun with your favorite applications without needing to be in a specific time or place.

Figure 4-3 Example of Changing From Handheld Mode to Tabletop Mode

This example shows a player having fun as he waits for a friend to arrive, using the Switch with the Joy-Con attached in handheld mode. After the friend arrives, the friends can now play together in tabletop mode.

The key for this scenario is that the player who was waiting for the friend did not need to set up anything in advance to enable them both to play. To play outside, they just needed to bring the Switch along in handheld mode.

With traditional handheld systems, you could only play with others if they each had the same gaming system. The Switch allows for multiplayer use even when the occasion suddenly presents itself.

Summary of Changing Play Modes

We believe that the need to switch play modes can happen while playing an application, due to changes in the user's environment.

One of the most important features of the Switch is its ability to switch between play modes at any point when there is a change in the user's environment.

For this reason, the game machine itself is designed to allow easy switching between modes with just the items that are included with the system.

Basically, we would like all applications to have the ability to switch between play modes to whatever the user desires, anytime, anywhere.
