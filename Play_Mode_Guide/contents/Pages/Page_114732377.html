1 Introduction

Objectives and Content

This section describes the three play modes available on the Nintendo Switch™ (hereafter Switch) and how to implement applications to work with those play modes.

In practical terms, it reviews the play modes and scenarios that are intended to offer players any kind of gaming experience, and provides a detailed definition of the term portable video game system.

Note

This document does not represent a set of guidelines. Not all applications need to be designed accordingly.

It is very important, however, that applications support the three play modes that the Switch offers.

To determine whether the Nintendo Switch system can accommodate the specifications for your application, make sure that you read this document when you start to plan and discuss your application.

Any guidelines that must be followed are also included in the Nintendo Switch Guidelines.

Intended Audience

This document is intended for planners and developers of Switch applications.

Structure of This Document

2 Targets for the Nintendo Switch describes the types of gaming experiences we want to provide to players.

3 Three Play Modes and 4 Changing Play Modes review the three play modes supported by the Switch, and provide examples for switching among the different play modes.

5 Nintendo Switch Application Basics and later sections describe the need for Switch software to run in all three modes: TV mode, tabletop mode, and handheld mode. They also describe how to plan for implementing these play modes in applications.
