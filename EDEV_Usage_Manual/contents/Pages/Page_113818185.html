9.1 EDEV Does Not Turn On

The EDEV might have stopped responding. Press and hold the POWER Button on the top of the EDEV for at least 12 seconds to turn the EDEV off, and then press the POWER Button again.

If the preceding steps do not turn the power on, the battery might not be sufficiently charged. Charge the EDEV as described in 5.1 Charging the EDEV.

If charging the EDEV does not resolve the issue, check the following.

Check the AC adapter you are using

Use the dedicated AC adapter for Switch (the HAC-002).

Check that you are using a correct charging method
Charging the EDEV by Connecting to an AC Adapter
  • Is the EDEV properly connected to the AC adapter?
  • Is the AC adapter plugged in?
Charging the EDEV by Attaching to the Nintendo Switch Dock
  • Is the EDEV properly attached to the Nintendo Switch dock?
  • Is the Nintendo Switch dock properly connected to the AC adapter?
  • Is the AC adapter plugged in?

If there were no problems with these connections, disconnect the AC adapter from the power outlet, wait 10 seconds, and then reconnect it.
