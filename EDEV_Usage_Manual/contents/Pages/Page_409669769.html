8.2 Initializing the EDEV

Update the EDEV firmware and initialize the EDEV.
When you initialize the EDEV, the installed applications and settings are reset.

Before you initialize the EDEV, follow the procedures described in 5.1 Charging the EDEV to make sure that the device is sufficiently charged.

For more information about initializing the EDEV, see Nintendo Switch EDEV Quick Start > Initializing the EDEV.

Note

To avoid malfunction, do not turn off the power to the EDEV or remove game cards or microSD cards while the EDEV is initializing. If the EDEV malfunctions, it cannot be restored and must be repaired by Nintendo.
