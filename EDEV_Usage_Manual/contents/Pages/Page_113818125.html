5.2 Turning the EDEV On and Off

This section describes how to turn power to the EDEV on and off.

Turning the EDEV On

  1. Press the POWER Button on the top of the EDEV.

    This turns the EDEV on.

    Note

    If you are using the EDEV for the first time, set it up as described in the Nintendo Switch EDEV Quick Start.


Turning the EDEV Off

  1. Press and hold down the POWER Button on the top of the EDEV for at least 7 seconds.

    This turns the EDEV off.
