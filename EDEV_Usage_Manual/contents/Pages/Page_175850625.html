6.1 Connecting the EDEV and PC

The EDEV may need to be connected to a PC when installing applications or writing to an NX development card.

The relay box is used to connect the EDEV to a PC.

The following actions are not supported while the EDEV is connected to the relay box because it is normally in TV mode.

  • Video output to the EDEV screen
  • Operation of a Joy-Con attached to the EDEV

To operate the EDEV while it is connected to a PC, connect the EDEV to a TV and use a detached Joy-Con, or switch the EDEV to handheld mode.

For more information about switching to handheld mode, see 8.4 Switching Between Handheld and TV Modes.

Note

If the EDEV is attached to the Nintendo Switch dock and the dock is connected to the relay box, the PC cannot recognize the EDEV. Remove the EDEV from the Nintendo Switch dock and connect it directly to the relay box.

You cannot use connected USB devices regardless of whether the relay box is connected to a PC.

The image shows the setup when the EDEV is connected to both a PC and a TV.

To create this setup, use the following procedure to connect the EDEV to a PC.

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the relay box.
    Connect the AC adapter to the AC adapter connector on the front of the relay box.

  2. Connect the relay box to the PC.
    Connect the USB Micro-B to Micro-A cable to the USB port on the front of the relay box and then connect it to the PC.

  3. Connect the relay box to the TV.
    Connect the HDMI cable to the HDMI output port on the front of the relay box and then connect it to the TV.

  4. Connect the relay box to the EDEV.
    Connect the relay box to the USB Type-C connector on the bottom of the EDEV.
