5.1 Charging the EDEV

To charge the EDEV, use any of the following methods.

  • Connect the AC adapter to the EDEV and recharge.
  • Connect the relay box to the EDEV and recharge.
  • Attach the EDEV to the Nintendo Switch dock and recharge.
Note

The EDEV does not recharge if it is simply connected to a PC via a USB cable.

Connecting the AC Adapter to the EDEV and Recharging
  1. Connect the AC adapter to the USB Type-C connector on the bottom of the EDEV.
  2. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The EDEV starts charging.

Connecting the Relay Box to the EDEV and Recharging

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the AC adapter connector on the front of the relay box.

  2. Connect the relay box to the USB Type-C connector on the bottom of the EDEV.

  3. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The EDEV starts charging.

Attaching the EDEV to the Nintendo Switch Dock and Recharging

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the Nintendo Switch dock.
    Open the cover on the back of the Nintendo Switch dock and connect the AC adapter to the top connector.

  2. Attach the EDEV to the Nintendo Switch dock.

  3. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The TV output LED illuminates and the EDEV starts charging.

    Info

    If the TV output LED on the Nintendo Switch dock does not illuminate, see 9.5 Nintendo Switch Dock Does Not Turn On.
