9.8 No TV Video and Audio Output

If there is no TV video or audio output, check the following.

  • Is the HDMI cable correctly connected to the Nintendo Switch dock or relay box?
  • Are the TV input settings correct?
  • Is the EDEV correctly connected to the Nintendo Switch dock or relay box?
  • Is the TV output LED flashing on the Nintendo Switch dock or relay box?
    If so, see 9.6 Flashing LED on the Nintendo Switch Dock.
  • Is the EDEV operating mode policy set to auto or console?

If you do not find any problems while checking these things, perform the following operations.

  1. Remove the EDEV from the Nintendo Switch dock or relay box.
  2. Restart the EDEV if no image appears on the EDEV LCD screen.
    For more information about restarting the EDEV, see 8.1 Restarting the EDEV.
  3. Confirm that an image appears on the EDEV LCD screen, and then connect the EDEV to the Nintendo Switch dock or relay box.
