6.2.3 Connecting USB Devices

Connect a USB device to the EDEV using one of the following procedures.

  • Connect the USB device via the Nintendo Switch dock
  • Connect the USB device directly to the EDEV
Connect the USB device via the Nintendo Switch dock

Connect a USB-Ethernet adapter to the Nintendo Switch dock to enable the use of communication features.

To connect a USB device to the EDEV via the Nintendo Switch dock, use the following procedure to connect the EDEV to the dock before supplying power to the dock.

  1. Turn the EDEV off.
    Press and hold down the POWER Button on the top of the EDEV for at least 7 seconds.

  2. Attach the EDEV to the Nintendo Switch dock.

  3. Connect a USB-Ethernet adapter to the Nintendo Switch dock.
    Open the cover on the back of the Nintendo Switch dock and connect the USB-Ethernet adapter to the middle connector.

  4. Connect the AC adapter to the Nintendo Switch dock.
    Connect the AC adapter to the uppermost connector.

    The power turns on to the Nintendo Switch dock and the TV output LED illuminates briefly.

    Info

    If the TV output LED on the Nintendo Switch dock does not illuminate, see 9.5 Nintendo Switch Dock Does Not Turn On.


  5. Press the POWER Button on the top of the EDEV.

    This turns the EDEV on.

Connect the USB device directly to the EDEV

When you connect a USB device to the USB Type-C port on the bottom of the EDEV console, the USB device is recognized regardless of whether the EDEV is on.

However, as with the Switch retail product, operation is not assured for devices connected to the EDEV console.
