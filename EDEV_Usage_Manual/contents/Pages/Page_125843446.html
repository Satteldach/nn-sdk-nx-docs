5.4 Turning the Nintendo Switch Dock On and Off

This section describes how to turn power to the Nintendo Switch dock on and off.

The power to the Nintendo Switch dock is turned on and off using the AC adapter.

Turning On the Nintendo Switch Dock

  1. Connect the AC adapter to the Nintendo Switch dock.
    Open the cover on the back of the Nintendo Switch dock and connect the AC adapter to the top connector.

  2. Connect the AC adapter to a power outlet.

    The power turns on to the Nintendo Switch dock and the TV output LED illuminates briefly.

    Info

    If the TV output LED on the Nintendo Switch dock does not illuminate, see 9.5 Nintendo Switch Dock Does Not Turn On.

Turning Off the Nintendo Switch Dock
  1. Unplug the AC adapter from the Nintendo Switch dock.
    Open the cover on the back of the Nintendo Switch dock and disconnect the AC adapter.

    This turns off the Nintendo Switch dock.
