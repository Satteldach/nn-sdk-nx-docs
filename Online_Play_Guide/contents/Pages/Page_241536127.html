2 Nintendo Switch Online Support

Nintendo Switch Online is a paid online service provided by Nintendo.
For more information about this service, see Nintendo Switch Online in the Nintendo Switch Overview.
This chapter describes the scope of use of Nintendo Switch Online by features in Switch applications, and how to support Nintendo Switch Online when developing applications.
If your application includes online features, be sure to read this section carefully.

In this chapter, online features that are available only to subscribers of Nintendo Switch Online are called member-only features, and features that can be used by everyone are called complimentary features.

Info:

If your application is for China, see 2.6 Support in Applications for China.
