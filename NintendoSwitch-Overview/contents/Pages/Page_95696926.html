Unsupported Devices

The following devices, which were supported by some past hardware, are not supported by Nintendo Switch.

  • Autostereoscopic LCD screen
  • Microphone (but an audio jack is provided)
  • RGB camera
  • Infrared communication

In addition, Switch does not come bundled with a stylus or a microSD card.
