5.1.1 Entrance

This screen is the welcome screen that is shown after turning on the system power or returning from sleep.

Figure 5-1 Entrance Screen Mockup


You can perform actions like resuming the application that was being played when the system was put to sleep or jumping to game news. For more information, see 5.2.3 News.

Screen Lock (Only When in Handheld Mode)

An Unlock screen appears after this Entrance screen to prevent erroneous operations.

This prevents situations like the accidental waking of a game while the Switch console is being carried around in a backpack.
