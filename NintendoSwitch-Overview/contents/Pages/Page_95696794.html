1 Introduction

Purpose and Content

The purpose of this document is to provide an overall understanding of the Nintendo Switch™ platform (Switch) by describing the Switch hardware, the system features, the development environment, and other aspects of the platform.

This document also introduces other documentation containing more detailed information.

Intended Audience

The intended audience of this document includes anyone involved in the development of Switch applications.

Markings

Switch systems and Nintendo Switch Docks that have the same product name may be distinguished by model number (such as [HAC-001] or [HAC-007]).

The model number is written after "MOD." on the back of the Switch system. You can also check them in Nintendo Switch Terminology.
