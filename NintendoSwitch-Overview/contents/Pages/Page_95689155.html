Components

Table 3-1 Descriptions of Each Component
Numbers Within the Figure
Name
Description
A POWER Button Operates the console sleep mode and turns the console on and off.
B Volume buttons Two buttons for adjusting the volume.
C Air vent The air vent for the console cooling fan.
D Audio jack The system does not have a built-in microphone. A headset must be connected to this jack to use features like voice chat.
E Game card slot A new game card slot. The game cards for previous hardware platforms, such as Nintendo 3DS, are not compatible, and cannot be inserted.
F Touch screen [HAC-001] [HAC-001(-01)]: 6.2-inch IPS LCD screen (1280 × 720).
[HEG-001]: 7.0-inch OLED screen (1280 × 720).
Equipped with a capacitive multi-touch panel. Up to 10 touch points can be detected at the same time.
G Brightness sensor A sensor to adjust the LCD brightness based on the ambient brightness.
H Speakers Two are provided.
[HAC-001] [HAC-001(-01)]: Speakers are equivalent to those on a New Nintendo 3DS.
[HEG-001]: Speakers have an improved sound pressure level over that of [HAC-001] and [HAC-001(-01)].
I USB Type-C™ connector Used to charge the Switch console and to connect to the Switch dock.
J microSD card slot One microSD card slot is provided. Supported card types include SD, SDHC, and SDXC.
K Stand Built-in stand that supports the Switch console so that it can stand on its own. Using this stand, the Joy-Con controllers can be detached and the Switch console stood up and used as a display for gameplay.
The [HEG-001] stand is freely adjustable.
L Air intake The air intake holes for the console cooling fan.
- NX-SoC

SoC equivalent to Tegra X1 from NVIDIA.
- Main memory [HAC-001] is equipped with 4 GB of LPDDR4, and [HAC-001(-01)] and [HEG-001] are equipped with 4 GB of LPDDR4X. This memory also serves as VRAM for displaying graphics on the screen. The application can use 3.2 GB of this memory.
- System memory [HAC-001] and [HAC-001(-01)] have 32 GB of storage, and the [HEG-001] has 64 GB of storage. Used to store the downloadable versions of applications and save data.
- Wireless

IEEE 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac and Bluetooth v4.1 are included.
Local communication uses a proprietary protocol based on IEEE 802.11 n.
- Accelerometer This sensor detects changes in inclination and motion.
- Gyroscope This sensor detects changes in angle and rotation speed.
- Cooling fan Fan to cool the device surface and internal components.
- Battery pack A 4310-mAh rechargeable battery is included. Battery life ranges from approximately 2.5 hours to 6.5 hours for [HAC-001] and 4.5 to 9.0 hours for [HAC-001(-01)] and [HEG-001].
Battery life varies depending on the usage conditions and the processing load of the application being played.
Note:

The following devices are deactivated when the Switch console is placed in the Switch dock.

  • Speakers
  • Built-in accelerometer and motion sensor
  • Video output to the Switch screen, and the touch screen
Note:

The following components, which were incorporated in the hardware to date for the Nintendo 3DS family and the Wii U GamePad, are not included in Nintendo Switch.

  • Autostereoscopic LCD screen
  • Microphone (audio jack is provided)
  • RGB camera
  • Infrared communication feature
