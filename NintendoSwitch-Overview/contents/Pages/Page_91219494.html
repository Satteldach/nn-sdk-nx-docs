5.2.5 Album

Screenshots and videos captured using the Capture Button are saved to Album in the HOME Menu. You can post those captured screenshots and video clips together with comments to certain social media sites.

Info:

Videos of the application gameplay can be captured by pressing and holding down the Capture Button. The scene captured in the video starts from a maximum of 30 seconds prior to pressing the Capture Button.

Info:
  • You can transfer screenshots and videos to a smart device by wirelessly connecting it with the Nintendo Switch system. For more information, see Nintendo Switch System Features Guide > System Application > Captures and Albums.
  • You can transfer screenshots and videos to a smart device or a computer by using a USB cable to connect the Nintendo Switch system with one of those devices. For more information, see Nintendo Switch System Features Guide > System Application > System Settings > Copy to PC via USB.
