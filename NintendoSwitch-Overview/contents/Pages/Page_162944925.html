Features of Nintendo Switch

Nintendo Switch is a console gaming device that you can take outside. It combines the power of a console that offers solid and substantive gameplay, with the flexibility of a handheld system that allows you to enjoy games wherever you can take them.

Nintendo Switch combines the advanced performance of a console with a size and weight that makes it easy to take anywhere, similar to a handheld system. With its high-resolution, large-format LCD screen that allows for touch operations, Switch offers a rich gaming experience, even when outside. Switch also offers the same number of buttons as previous platforms. Though it has a large LCD screen, the Switch console is approximately 13.9 mm thick, and only weighs around 398 grams with the controllers (described later) attached for use as a handheld system. When Switch is being used as a console, TV output uses the Nintendo Switch dock that comes bundled with the system. Switching between TV mode and handheld mode is as simple as attaching and detaching the system to and from the dock. The same game can be played anywhere.

Nintendo Switch comes with a pair of controllers collectively called the Joy-Con™. These are independent left and right controllers. Holding the Joy-Con controllers in different ways, and making different uses of the built-in sensors and features, opens up a variety of new ways of gameplay.

Features of Nintendo Switch Lite

Nintendo Switch Lite is a dedicated handheld version of the Switch system.

Compared to Nintendo Switch, the controllers cannot be removed and the device is smaller and lighter, which makes it easier for people to take with them on the go. Because it is designed uniquely for handheld mode, the hardware does not include video output capability, and as a result it cannot display content on a television.

The same applications that run on Nintendo Switch can also run on Nintendo Switch Lite. Although Switch Lite cannot be used in TV mode, it can be used for gameplay in tabletop mode because you can connect Joy-Con or Pro Controllers wirelessly and use them while watching the action on the screen.
Note that Joy-Con controllers cannot be charged using the Switch Lite system. If you plan to use Joy-Con controllers with Switch Lite, you also need a separate charger, like the Joy-Con charging grip.

Nintendo Switch – OLED Model Features

The OLED model is an improved version of the original Nintendo Switch (hereafter referred to as the original model).

This model offers all three play modes: TV mode, tabletop mode, and handheld mode. It is positioned along with the original model as the primary hardware of the Switch platform.

Figure 2-3 Nintendo Switch – OLED Model

The console, which is almost the same size as the original model, is equipped with a 7.0-inch OLED screen made possible by narrower bezels. It enables games to be enjoyed on a large and vivid screen.
It also has the following features.

  • The adjustable stand on the rear of the console is wide and the angle can be freely adjusted, enabling it to stand freely at any angle when playing in tabletop mode.
  • With an improved sound pressure level, the new speaker provides clearer sound when playing in handheld or tabletop mode.
  • 64 GB of system memory provides room to download more applications.
  • The included dock is equipped with a LAN port, increasing the reliability of online play in TV mode even without the optional wired LAN adapter.

The same applications that run on the original model can also run on the OLED model. Play is fundamentally the same as on the original model.

Positioning Switch Lite Within the Switch Family

Switch and Switch Lite have the following positioning within the Switch platform. Collectively, Switch (original model and OLED model) and Switch Lite are referred to as the Nintendo Switch family.

Switch is positioned as the primary device of the Switch family, because its hardware allows for gameplay in all play modes: TV mode, tabletop mode, and handheld mode. Conversely, Switch Lite is positioned as a Switch specialized for the handheld mode experience. It is designed to meet the demand of users who want a Switch that is lighter and easier to carry around because they will not be playing in TV mode.

To enable applications for the Switch platform to be enjoyed on both Switch and Switch Lite, we recommend supporting all three play modes: TV mode, tabletop mode, and handheld mode. However, it is not a requirement that games support handheld mode and be playable on Switch Lite. For more information about play modes, see Nintendo Switch Play Mode Guide.
