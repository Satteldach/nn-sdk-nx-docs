7 Nintendo Switch Online

Nintendo Switch Online is a paid service that lets members play online with their Switch consoles and gives them access to special features like the use of a dedicated smartphone app.
Members who have subscribed to Nintendo Switch Online can use the service.
We also offer the Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack plan, which allows use of additional content beyond the basic five services.

Details are available on the Nintendo website for the following regions.
Japan: https://www.nintendo.co.jp/hardware/switch/onlineservice/
North America: https://www.nintendo.com/switch/online-service/
Europe: https://www.nintendo.co.uk/Nintendo-Switch/Nintendo-Switch-Online/Nintendo-Switch-Online-1183143.html

Info:

For more information about the various features of the online service and the necessary request processes, see Nintendo Switch Online Play Guide > Nintendo Switch Online Support. If your application has internet communication features, be sure to read the Nintendo Switch Online Play Guide.

Services for Nintendo Switch Online Subscribers

The following services are available to members of Nintendo Switch Online.

  • Online Play
    Multiplayer options, including online matches and cooperative play.
  • Nintendo Entertainment System – Nintendo Switch Online / Super Nintendo Entertainment System – Nintendo Switch Online / Game Boy – Nintendo Switch Online
    Exclusive software, featuring a collection of nostalgic NES, SNES, and Game Boy games.
  • Smartphone App
    The Nintendo Switch Online smartphone app is dedicated to the enhancement of online play.
    It enables the use of voice chat and services linked to games.
    For more information, see Nintendo Switch Voice Chat Guide.
  • Save Data Cloud
    See 7.1 Save Data Cloud.
  • Special Offers
    Opportunities to buy exclusive products, receive exclusive items (for use in specific applications), and use exclusive discount services.
Info:

The following options are available even without a subscription to Nintendo Switch Online.

  • Using Nintendo eShop
  • Adding and managing friends
  • Sharing captured game screens in social media
  • Using Nintendo Switch parental controls
  • Competitive and cooperative play via local communication
  • Receiving news
  • Receiving data via the BCAT Data Delivery System
  • Receiving patches
  • System updates

Services for Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack Subscribers

Membership in the Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion PackSwitch Online + Expansion Pack plan not only provides access to the services available to members of Nintendo Switch Online, but also to the following Expansion Pack content.

  • Nintendo 64 – Nintendo Switch Online
    Access to a collection of exclusive Nintendo 64 titles.
  • Game Boy Advance - Nintendo Switch Online
    Access to a collection of exclusive Game Boy Advance titles.
  • SEGA Genesis – Nintendo Switch Online
    Access to a collection of exclusive SEGA Genesis titles.
  • Paid DLC
    Access to paid DLC for specific applications.

Countries Where Nintendo eShop Is Not Available

In countries where Nintendo eShop is not available, none of the features described earlier in Services for Nintendo Switch Online Subscribers are available to Switch users because there is no method for subscribing to Nintendo Switch Online.
For more information about countries where Nintendo eShop is not available, see Nintendo Switch E-Commerce Guide > List of Countries Where Nintendo eShop Is Available.
