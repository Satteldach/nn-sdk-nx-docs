3 NFC Touchpoint Icon

This section contains guidelines and considerations for using the NFC touchpoint icon.

Intended Uses

If the amiibo must come into contact with the New Nintendo 3DS, display the NFC touchpoint on the lower screen of the New Nintendo 3DS.

Minimum Margin

The NFC touchpoint must be surrounded by a minimum margin. As a general rule, this margin may not contain images or text.
The minimum margin may be compromised when the display space is limited.
The minimum margin is included in the distributed data as a clear rectangle. See the resource for details.

Minimum Margin (Red Line)

Color, Background Color, and Effects

There are no specifications for the color or background color of the NFC touchpoint except that it must be visible.
Any colors may be used.

Notes

You can add a drop shadow or outer glow effect to ensure visibility.
A text outline is generally prohibited.
