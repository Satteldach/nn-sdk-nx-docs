Rules for Using Artwork

Although the artwork included in this package can be used as is for purposes such as Nintendo Switch and Nintendo 3DS family applications and related printed materials, the composition of colors and lines in this artwork can also be tailored to fit the brand concept and look of each game.

The following are examples of such alterations.

  • Changing to more appropriate colors, or adding patterns
  • Adding shadows, glow effects, textures, or borders as needed
  • Simplifying the artwork by using just its outline, or removing the outline entirely
  • Changing to a more digital or hand-drawn feel

However, you must ensure that such arrangements do not confuse users.

The following are examples of arrangements that could confuse users.

  • The artwork is arranged in a way that causes the user to misjudge the size of the product.
    Example) The Joy-Con appear larger than the Nintendo Switch Pro Controller when they are both shown together in the same space. (Obviously, the Joy-Con are smaller.)
  • Making a product appear as a different one by removing or changing the elements that are unique to that product, making it hard to understand what the drawing represents.
    One example would be removing the rails from the Switch main unit and the Joy-Con controllers to make it resemble a Wii U GamePad.

Illustrations are available in both the RGB and CMYK color modes. Use the RGB versions when working with applications or websites. Use the CMYK version when working with printed materials.

Note

If anything about using or arranging the illustrations is unclear, or if you want to use a particular effect, contact your Nintendo representative.

For Switch

These images are for the Joy-Con™, Nintendo Switch Pro Controller, and both the Joy-Con and Nintendo Switch Pro Controller.
They are used for the Switch LCD screen or TV display.

Info
  • Use the touchpoint locator image suitable for the connected controller. Although it is possible to show multi-use indicators for the Joy-Con controllers and Nintendo Switch Pro Controllers, we recommend showing the image for either the Joy-Con or the Nintendo Switch Pro Controller based on which controller is connected.
  • The position of the lit player LED can be changed by switching the layer. In the application, use the images where the appropriate player LEDs are lit according to the number of the controller to which the amiibo is loaded.
  • Although it is possible to use images with all player LEDs turned off, we recommend that you use images without turning the player LEDs off.
