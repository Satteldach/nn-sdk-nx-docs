2101-0001 nn::i2c::ResultNoAck No Ack from i2c. Either a No Ack was explicitly returned from the destination device, or there is an issue with the destination device. Restart the console. If this error occurs repeatedly, a certain chip inside the console may be defective.
2101-0002 nn::i2c::ResultBusBusy The i2c bus is in a busy state. This error can occur when the device you are attempting to connect to is not working, or when the system has entered a state of extremely high load. Restart the console. If this error occurs repeatedly, a certain chip inside the console may be defective.
